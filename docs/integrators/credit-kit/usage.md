# Using the SlicerX credit

You can build your product on SlicerX and ship it under your own name. When you do, credit SlicerX with the badge or the line in `copy.md`. This page covers how. It is a plain summary of `TRADEMARK.md` in the SlicerX repository, which has the full wording.

## Do

- Use one of the badges, or the line "Made possible by SlicerX", and link it to https://slicerx.app/support.
- Say your product is "made possible by SlicerX" or "built on SlicerX".
- Keep the credit on your About screen or page, and in your docs. A site footer is a good place for the badge or the Support button.
- Pick the badge size and the dark or light variant that suits the page.

## Don't

- Use the SlicerX name, the wordmark or the layered X mark as your product's name, logo or app icon. The badge credits SlicerX; it does not brand your product.
- Say or suggest that SlicerX makes, endorses or supports your product.
- Change the badge or the button: no new colors, no stretching, no cropping, no added effects, no rewording. Use the variants in this kit as they are, scaled evenly if you need a different size.
- Put the credit in your app bar, splash screen or store listing title.

If you are not sure a use is fine, ask the SlicerX project before you ship it.
