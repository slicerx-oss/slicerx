### Added

- `slicerx_design_trace_image` 1.0.0 traces a PNG or JPEG into a finished sketch in the design timeline of the project open in the app, as Trace image in the sketch tool does. `image` is `{path}` (a .png, .jpg or .jpeg inside the directories the server may read) or `{base64}`, at most 30 MB. The image is traced on the server, so its size is not limited by the app link; the loops then go to the timeline as `slicerx_design_sketch` sends them. `mode` picks the inside: `auto` (opaque areas when the image has transparency, else the dark side of an automatic split), `dark` or `light` against `threshold` (0 to 255, automatic when absent), `color` near any of `colors` (up to 8, `#rrggbb`) within `color_tolerance` (0 to 100, 12 when absent), or `alpha`. Specks and pinholes under `speck_mm2` (0.5 when absent) are cleaned away. Edges become lines, arcs, circles and splines within `smoothing_mm` (0.01 to 10, half a working pixel when absent); `fit` is `lines`, `arcs` or `curves` (the default), and a turn sharper than `corner_angle_deg` (1 to 179, 50 when absent) is a corner. One of `width_mm`, `height_mm` or `mm_per_px` sizes the trace (50 mm wide when none is given), `center` places its middle in sketch mm (when absent, the middle of the plane, as for `slicerx_design_sketch_svg`: the middle of the app's bed on the bed, the frame's origin on a plane or face), `frame` puts it on a plane or face as for `slicerx_design_sketch`, and `max_side_px` (16 to 4096, 2048 when absent) is the size the image is shrunk to before tracing. `dry_run` traces and reports without adding anything, so a client can tune the settings first; `preview_image` also returns a PNG of the image with the loops drawn over it. The reply has the sketch `id` and `name` (none on a dry run), the `loops`, `counts` of lines, arcs, splines and circles, `sizeMm`, `mmPerPx`, `imagePx`, `workingPx`, `dropped` (specks and pinholes cleaned away), the `mode` used and its `threshold`, the sketch `check` and `warnings`. A trace of more than 256 loops, or loops over the 63.5 KB the app link carries, or a spline that cannot be drawn, is refused with the setting to raise. Pass the id as `sketch` to `slicerx_geom_extrude` or `slicerx_geom_revolve`.
