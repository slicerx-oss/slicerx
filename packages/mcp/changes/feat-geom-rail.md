### Added

- `slicerx_geom_rail` 1.0.0 adds a rail (a railing or fence) to a design in one call: a round post at every corner of a line (each turn sharper than 30 degrees, and an open line's ends), the rest spread evenly between corners no more than `spacing_mm` apart, and a flat top bar on them. The line is `path` (points on a plane, `closed` for a rail all round) or `face` (all round the outer edge of a flat face, `inset_mm` in from it). Posts and bar default to twice the nozzle, with the bar 0.2 mm wider than the posts so it caps them; thinner posts or bars, a bar not wider than the posts, or a rail no taller than its bar are refused with the size that works. The posts sink 0.05 mm into what they stand on. The rail is a new part of its own (`name`, `slot`) or, with `join`, part of the part it stands on. It adds two steps, "Rail posts, N" and "Rail bar", that `slicerx_design_undo` takes off together; a bar that does not go through takes the posts back with it. The reply's `rail` gives the part, the number of posts and how many stand on corners, the largest spacing between posts, the length and the sizes used.
