### Changed

- `slicerx_geom_sweep` 1.6.0 (past 1.5.0, the refusal that says why) sweeps a profile with holes: a loop inside another is a hole, and its wall runs the whole length of the path, so a tube, a spout with its bore or a hollow handle is one sweep. The holes taper and turn with the profile when `scale` and `twist_deg` are given. A hole that touches or crosses the outer loop is refused naming the two loops, and `thicken` with a holed profile is refused, since the holes already make the walls. Before, a profile with a hole was refused with "a sweep profile has no holes yet". The `profile` field's description says inner loops are holes: "Loops (inner ones are holes) and frame as revolve takes; plane across the path's start; open: open chain".
