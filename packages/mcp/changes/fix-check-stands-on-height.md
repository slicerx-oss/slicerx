### Fixed

- `slicerx_design_check` 1.24.3 (from 1.24.2): each "stands on" failure now gives the height of the top the part was measured against, so a fix can build from it without reading the support. On a support whose top rises or curves under the part, it is the lowest point of that top under the part: "build it from the top of Body (loft), whose lowest point under it is at z 15.26 mm". On any other support it is the support's highest point: "build it from the top of Base, whose highest point is at z 17.6 mm". The height is rounded to 0.01 mm, like the other numbers in the sentence, and is the same value the check measures the base against. Before, a part that floated 0.34 mm over a loft whose top curves up toward one end was told only to "build it from the top of Body (loft)", with no height to build to. What passes and fails is unchanged.
