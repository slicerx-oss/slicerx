### Fixed

- `slicerx_geom_loft` 1.13.3 (from 1.13.2) keeps each corner of a mesh loft matched to the same corner from section to section, for profiles drawn with arcs, ellipses or splines and no landmarks. They are matched where their pieces meet, as the exact loft matches them, and points that land on an arc are put back on its circle. Before, their points were matched by count, so where profiles flattened to different counts a corner ran across a side: a lofted body's top edge went soft between its sections, its side pulled in, and a lofted body drawn with arcs dipped under the bed at its lowest line. The answer's fields are the same; such lofts give different meshes, and their volumes and triangle counts can change (a loft through rounded rectangles went from 46907.84 to 46935.40 mm³ and from 20156 to 12476 triangles). A point given a little off the side of such a loft for `slicerx_geom_hole` moves onto it by about the distance the exact solid gives: 0.226 mm on a lofted body at x 40, where it read 0.243 mm and the exact solid gives 0.228 mm. Profiles of straight lines alone, and profiles with landmarks, loft as before.
