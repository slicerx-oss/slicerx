### Added

- `slicerx_printer_set_slot` 1.0.0 asks a person to set one filament slot, such as an AMS slot: `setting` `{slot, material, color, filamentId, nozzleTempMin, nozzleTempMax}` (`material` one of the listed filament types, any case; `color` `#rrggbb`; `filamentId` the slot's name, which is the printer's filament preset id, 1 to 16 characters; nozzle range 150 to 350 °C, low first), with an optional `reason` shown on the card. The hub checks the setting against the printer and raises a card; a person approves in the app on the computer, and a phone away from home never shows it, and neither the client nor `slicerx_approve` can answer it. Once approved, the hub writes the slot as the app's own slot write does. The answer is `status` `needs_person` with `request_id`; `slicerx_pending_approvals` reports `done` or `failed`. Without `setting` it reads the slots back (`slots`, `settable`, `reason`). Refusals: `invalid_input`, `not_found`, `not_supported` (a printer that takes no slot setting, or simulated printers with `reason` `no_hub`), `bad_state` while a print runs, and `refused` for a spool with an RFID tag.
- The hub takes slot work (`approvals.register` `work` `{kind: "slot", printerId, setting}`, action `printer.adjust` hashed as `{printerId, slot: setting}`) from agent and partner connections. A partner app may raise it and never answers it.
