### Added

- `slicerx_design_edit_step` 1.5.0 (from 1.4.0) takes `section` for a loft step: reshape a lofted body (a car body, a fuselage, a bottle, a vase) by its stations. Change one station's width, height or place along the run (`station` with `width`, `height`, `scale`, `at`, `out`), add a station blended from the two around it (`add`) or take one out (`remove`), raise or drop the top or bottom line toward the ends (`line`), lean an end out (`end` with `lean`, `leanBy` or `overhang`), give the body more or less curve (`curve`) or a flatter bottom (`flatten`). Stations are named front, back, middle, widest, shoulder or all, and nose, tip, tail, rear, neck and base work too. Extrudes joined on the body's top and parts standing on it are moved up or down onto the new top at the steps that made them, and their paint goes with them. The answer lists what moved, and says when a part now hangs past an edge, sits into the body or stands clear of it, and when the body now needs supports. The edit is kept either way.
- `slicerx_design_steps` 1.3.0 (from 1.2.0) lists a loft step's stations front to back (number, place, width, height) and its run axis. When the design has a loft, the answer carries `shapeWords`: phrases people use for a body's shape ("fuller nose", "more overhang", "rounder shoulder") and the section edit for each.
