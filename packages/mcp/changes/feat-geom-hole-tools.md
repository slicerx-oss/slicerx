### Breaking

- `slicerx_geom_hole` 2.0.0 (from 1.3.0) cuts a new round hole in a model or a design. Changing a hole already there moved to `slicerx_geom_hole_edit` (see Added). Place the hole by `center`, its center on the flat face it starts at or any point on its axis line, with `axis` either way round (left out, square to the face at `center`), or by `face`, `{at, normal?}`, a point on that face for a hole square to it. `diameter_mm` is required. Without `depth_mm` the hole goes through the wall it starts in; `counterbore` or `countersink` puts a head at its entry. The hole starts where its axis line meets the part nearest the point, and its whole circle, and the head's, must sit on a flat face square to the axis: a point on a round face (a boss's side), a circle that runs off the face, an axis more than 1 degree off square, a line that meets no part and a hole already on that axis are each refused with `invalid_input`, the last naming the hole and `slicerx_geom_hole_edit`, and nothing changes. The answer has `hole`, the hole as made (`center`, `axis`, `diameterMm`, `depthMm`, `kind`, `head`), in place of `before` and `after`. On a design the hole is a `shape.extrude` step, a circle on the entry face cut into it as Extrude with Cut makes it, named by `label` (default `Hole <diameter> mm`), and a head is a second cut on the same face after it, named `Counterbore <diameter> mm` or `Countersink <diameter> mm`: a wider circle as deep as the counterbore, or for a countersink a circle narrowing to the hole, its sides drafted by half the cone's angle. A countersink's `angle_deg` is 30 to 90 (default 90), and its cone must end above the hole's far end. When the head cannot go on, the cut is taken back off and the call is refused. With a model it writes the new STL.

### Added

- `slicerx_geom_hole_edit` 1.0.0 changes a round hole already in a model or a design: a new diameter, a blind hole's depth, and a counterbore or a countersink at its entry. It takes the input that version 1.3.0 of `slicerx_geom_hole` took and gives the same answer, except that a pick that finds no hole now says a new hole is `slicerx_geom_hole`'s, and the `operationResult` of an edit by a topology reference names `geom.hole_edit`. With the app link on it runs in the open app as the `geom.hole_edit` method, edit class.
