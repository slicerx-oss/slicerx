### Added

- `slicerx_geom_fit_check` 1.0.0 measures the gaps between a design's bodies for print-in-place parts (hinges, pins, chains), with the engine's fit check. It takes `design`, optionally `part` (part indexes, each once, default all), `material` (a filament type such as PLA, PETG or TPU) and `layer_height_mm` (0.04 to 0.8 mm), and returns `limits` (`side_mm` and `vertical_mm`, the smallest gaps moving pieces keep per side for the nozzle, layer height and material, by the same fit rule as `fit_check`, with `material`, `nozzle_mm` and `layer_height_mm`), `pieces` (bodies that touch count as one), `bodies` (`body`, `part`, `name`), `gaps` and `warnings`. Two bodies can have a side gap and a gap above or below, each held to its own limit, so a tight gap over a pin is not hidden by a wider one beside it. A side gap within 0.02 mm under its limit counts as clearing it: a round pin and bore drawn as facets measure a little closer than they are modeled. So does a gap above or below between round faces, such as over a pin lying flat in its bore, even when the bore is drawn with few sides and the level strip along its top is over 1 mm wide. A level strip is the top or bottom of a round face only when the facets past it on both sides keep turning, a second one leaning further within 2 mm, so a pin or bore drawn in as few as 8 sides counts as round, while a strip with a chamfer on both sides, such as a drafted rib or a lead-in, is a flat face. A gap above or below under a flat face, over a level one (a rail or rib narrower than 1 mm counts), is held to its limit itself, since the fit rule rounds it down to whole layers. A strip under 1 mm wide on the face above is no flat face, even beside a wider face across a thin groove. Whether a face is flat does not depend on how the part is turned. Each gap has `bodies`, `names`, `kind` (`fused` for a touch or overlap, `horizontal` or `vertical` under the limit, `apart` for the closest gap of two pieces within 2 mm), `direction` (`xy` or `z`), `gap_mm`, `limit_mm` (the limit for its direction), `verdict` (`fuses`, `binds` or `slides`), `where` for a body resting on another ("on the top face of Tray"), and `from` and `to`. Each warning says what to change, such as "Lid and Tray touch on the top face of Tray, 0.0 mm; moving pieces need 0.4 mm or more". Read only. Its description says that `slicerx_design_check` of a plan with `printInPlace` waits for it. App link method `geom.fit_check` (read class).

### Changed

- `slicerx_design_put_plan` 1.1.0 takes `printInPlace` in the plan: the names of the pieces that print assembled and move (at least two), each its own body, such as Lid and Tray, Sun and Planet 1, or Segment 1 to Segment 8. Its description asks for them when pieces of the part print assembled and move (hinges, chains, gears, jointed, articulated or flexi segments).
- `slicerx_design_check` 1.1.0 adds two items for a plan with `printInPlace`, and a third when that plan sets a gap: `pieces` (as many separate pieces as the plan names, with the plan's names; a fused or merged piece fails with what to do, such as "Tail and Body are one piece; keep them as separate bodies", and so does a name no body has, unless the bodies left over are bodies of one part, such as a mirrored leaf, which take the names left over, one body each, when the count is right and their part answers no other name; when that part answers a piece's name too, the item says to build the part under a name that is no piece's, and the first such miss of a plan does not use its fix round; when the count is right and each body left over is a part of its own, the item names each one beside the plan name it misses, such as "Segment 1 head is not named Segment 1", and that first miss does not use the fix round either) and `gaps` (every gap between moving pieces at least the fit rule's limit for the nozzle, layer height and material, and every piece within 2 mm of another; a miss names the pieces and the gap, such as "Lid and Tray touch on the top face of Tray, 0.0 mm; this print-in-place plan needs 0.4 mm or more"), and `planned gaps` (each size the plan names as a gap, such as `print_gap` or `clearance_vertical`, at least the same limit for its way: side by side, above or below, or the larger of the two when its name says neither, since such a gap is on every face; a miss gives the gap to use, such as "print_gap of Spinner: gap 0.3 mm (vertical) is under the 0.4 mm this printer needs between moving parts; use 0.4 mm"). Pieces made by `slicerx_geom_array` with `merge` false also answer the name their place in the row gives them, so a plan naming Segment 1 to Segment 9, or Planet 1 to Planet 3, matches copies named Segment 1 2 to Segment 1 9, or Planet 1 2 and Planet 1 3. The body read back has `fit` with the pieces, bodies (with `array`, the part copied as `of` and the place in the row as `n`, for a body an array of separate copies made or copied) and gaps (`flat` on a gap above or below under a flat face). Plans without `printInPlace` get the same items as before. A plan with `printInPlace` is checked only after `slicerx_geom_fit_check` has run on the design as it is now, over the planned pieces (each piece's own part when every piece names one, else every part); until then the check is refused, says to run it, and uses no check. A plan the person made in the mimir tab whose sizes they changed on a sheet after approving it is refused until a new plan is answered, and still refused after that answer, approved or declined, since its sizes are not the ones the person confirmed. In the mimir tab these refusals name no tool or plan field. The description says when a plan is refused. Its short description for a local model says that a plan with `printInPlace` also gets pieces and gaps (gap sizes in the plan too), checked only after the fit check has run on the design as it is now. The minimum wall no longer counts a ray that crosses a gap or grazes a face, so a gap between bodies or a sliver facet at a bore's end no longer reads as a thin wall.
- `slicerx_design_new` 1.1.0 refuses to start a design under the name of an open design whose part already passed `slicerx_design_check` against its plan, and names that design: change it instead, or give a second part another name. Its description says so.
- `slicerx_geom_array` with a `design` and `merge` false records the part it copied on the step that adds the copies (`arrayOf`, saved with the design's history, when the part's name is 120 characters or fewer, as the project file keeps it), which `slicerx_design_check` reads to match the copies to a plan's names.
