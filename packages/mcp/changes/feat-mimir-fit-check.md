### Added

- `slicerx_geom_fit_check` 1.0.0 measures the gaps between a design's bodies for print-in-place parts (hinges, pins, chains), with the engine's fit check. It takes `design`, optionally `part` (part indexes, each once, default all), `material` (a filament type such as PLA, PETG or TPU) and `layer_height_mm` (0.04 to 0.8 mm), and returns `limits` (`side_mm` and `vertical_mm`, the smallest gaps moving pieces keep per side for the nozzle, layer height and material, by the same fit rule as `fit_check`, with `material`, `nozzle_mm` and `layer_height_mm`), `pieces` (bodies that touch count as one), `bodies` (`body`, `part`, `name`), `gaps` and `warnings`. Two bodies can have a side gap and a gap above or below, each held to its own limit, so a tight gap over a pin is not hidden by a wider one beside it. A side gap within 0.02 mm under its limit counts as clearing it: a round pin and bore drawn as facets measure a little closer than they are modeled. A gap above or below is held to its limit itself, since the fit rule rounds it down to whole layers. Each gap has `bodies`, `names`, `kind` (`fused` for a touch or overlap, `horizontal` or `vertical` under the limit, `apart` for the closest gap of two pieces within 2 mm), `direction` (`xy` or `z`), `gap_mm`, `limit_mm` (the limit for its direction), `verdict` (`fuses`, `binds` or `slides`), `where` for a body resting on another ("on the top face of Tray"), and `from` and `to`. Each warning says what to change, such as "Lid and Tray touch on the top face of Tray, 0.0 mm; moving pieces need 0.4 mm or more". Read only. App link method `geom.fit_check` (read class).

### Changed

- `slicerx_design_put_plan` 1.1.0 takes `printInPlace` in the plan: the names of the pieces that print assembled and move (at least two), each its own body.
- `slicerx_design_check` 1.1.0 adds two items for a plan with `printInPlace`: `pieces` (as many separate pieces as the plan names, with the plan's names; a fused or merged piece fails with what to do, such as "Tail and Body are one piece; keep them as separate bodies", and so does a name no body has, unless the bodies left over are bodies of one part, such as a mirrored leaf, which take the names left over when the count is right) and `gaps` (every gap between moving pieces at least the fit rule's limit for the nozzle, layer height and material, and every piece within 2 mm of another; a miss names the pieces and the gap, such as "Lid and Tray touch on the top face of Tray, 0.0 mm; this print-in-place plan needs 0.4 mm or more"). The body read back has `fit` with the pieces, bodies and gaps. Plans without `printInPlace` get the same items as before. The minimum wall no longer counts a ray that crosses a gap or grazes a face, so a gap between bodies or a sliver facet at a bore's end no longer reads as a thin wall.
