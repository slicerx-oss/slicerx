### Changed

- `slicerx_design_put_plan` 1.4.0 takes `amends` and `reason`, so you can change the sizes of a plan of yours once its part is built. Put the plan again with the same `planId`, set `amends` to that planId and give `reason`, a sentence on why the build changes it (required, at most 200 characters). Only sizes can change: the part, units, features (names, kinds, size keys and kinds of each size), whether there is an overall size, and the rows of holes (names and counts) must stay as they are, and the plan's assumptions and questions are kept. An amend is refused for a plan with no design tied to it ("the plan's part is not built yet"), for one amended once already with no failed check since ("build to it as changed"), for one whose fix round is over (after the `stop` result), for a planId the person made in the mimir tab, when `amends` and `plan.planId` differ, when `design` names another design, when no size differs, and when `reason` is missing or empty; `reason` without `amends` is refused too. The plan keeps its design, its failed checks and its fix round, so an amend never buys a second round. The result gives `revision` (2 for the first amend), `changes` (each size changed, with `where`: feature, overall or hole, `feature`, `size`, `from` and `to`) and `underFloors` for the amended plan, kept as given as a new plan is. The summary names each change, such as "Base height 30 mm to 34 mm".
- `slicerx_design_check` 1.6.0 gives `revision` in its result and "(plan revision 2)" in its summary when it checks an amended plan. Other results are unchanged.
