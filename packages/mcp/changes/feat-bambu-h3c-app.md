### Added

- `slicerx_project_set_tool` 1.0.0: puts a project filament on a tool of a printer whose hotends are its tools (the Bambu Lab H3C, nine on one extruder), or gives every filament back to the slicer's pick with `tool` `auto`. 2.85 mm TPU stays on the TPU tool and no other filament may take it; a tool the printer lacks is refused too. Answers with the project, whose `hotends` read it back.

### Changed

- `slicerx_app_plate_settings` and `slicerx_app_plate_add` 1.1.0 take `hotendMap` (tools from 1, index slot - 1) on the H3C. The app refuses a tool the printer lacks, 2.85 mm TPU off the TPU tool and any other filament on it, with `invalid_input`.
- `slicerx_app_plates`, `slicerx_app_plate_show`, `slicerx_app_plate_settings`, `slicerx_app_plate_add`, `slicerx_app_plate_duplicate`, `slicerx_app_plate_rename`, `slicerx_app_plate_remove` and `slicerx_app_plate_move` 1.1.0 read back `hotends` on the shown plate of an H3C: `auto`, and per filament in use `slot`, `tool` (from 1), `hotend` (from 0), `tpuTool` and `tpu285`.
- `slicerx_project_show` 1.2.0 returns `hotends` on the H3C: `auto`, and per filament `slot`, `tool`, `hotend`, `tpu_tool` and `tpu285`. Before a slice it is the slicer's rule with every filament counted as printed, or the project's own map.
- `slicerx_slice_file` 1.7.0 and `slicerx_estimate_file` 1.5.0 return `filament_map` (`extruders`, `nozzles`, `auto`) with the sx engine on a printer with a filament map, and `hotends` on the H3C. On a catalog printer with a chamber limit they send it to the engine, so a chamber temperature over 65 C on `machine:bambu-h3c` prints at 65 C with a `safety_limit` warning (`chamber_temperature_clamped`). `process:<tier>` on `machine:bambu-h3c` carries the maker's process values per hotend type (`print_extruder_variant` with Standard, High Flow and TPU285, `print_extruder_id` and a value per type in each list), so a part on the TPU or a High Flow hotend prints with that hotend's values.
- `slicerx_slice_file` 1.7.0 and `slicerx_estimate_file` 1.5.0 with `project_settings` slice an H3C project on the tools it sets by hand (`filament_map_mode` Manual with `filament_nozzle_map`, hotends from 0). A filament the map leaves unset gets the slicer's pick, a hotend of its kind that no other filament has. Before, they took the slicer's pick for every filament.
- `slicerx_slice` 2.4.0 returns `hotends` per plate on the H3C, and holds a project's chamber temperature to the printer's limit as `slicerx_slice_file` does.
