### Changed

- `slicerx_geom_sketch_check` 1.1.0, `slicerx_geom_extrude` 1.3.0, `slicerx_geom_revolve` 1.3.0 and `slicerx_design_sketch` 1.2.0 take splines in sketch loops: a segment `{"type":"spline","points":[[u,v],...],"to":[u,v]}` runs a smooth curve from where it starts through `points` to `to`, and a loop `{"type":"spline","points":[[u,v],...]}` (3 or more points) is a smooth closed curve. The curve passes through every point (a centripetal Catmull-Rom spline), and the engine flattens it within 0.005 mm, half the default slice resolution, so a design replays to the same mesh whatever profile is picked. Design steps and finished sketches store the points, not the flattened curve. Spline bodies are built on the mesh path for now. A spline that bends too sharply to draw within 0.005 mm, or a loop or sketch with too many points once drawn, is refused with the new `tooDense` issue kind.
- `slicerx_geom_loft` and `slicerx_geom_sweep` take spline profiles, and `slicerx_geom_sweep` takes a path with spline segments: its ends are square to the curve where it starts and ends. A closed spline loop as a sweep path is refused, as a circle is.
- `slicerx_design_sketch` 1.2.0: constraints name a spline point as `{e, at: "point", seg, i}` (`{e, at: "point", i}` on a closed spline loop) and take point constraints on it; a constraint that names a spline as a curve is refused. The summary says how many splines a sketch has. A spline with no `to`, no points or too few, a spline the engine cannot draw (checked before and after the constraints are solved), and constraints the solve would leave out are refused before anything reaches the timeline.
- `slicerx_geom_sketch_edit` 1.1.0 mirrors and patterns splines as their points and refuses intersect, trim and extend on a sketch with a spline.
- The sketch tools other than `slicerx_geom_revolve` describe their loops in one line and parse them with the same schema, reading each loop by the form its keys name, so an error names the field (`loops.2.segments.0.to`) and a loop with another form's fields, such as a circle with `segments`, is refused rather than read as that form.
