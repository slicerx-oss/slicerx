### Changed

- `slicerx_design_check` 1.13.0 (from 1.12.0) adds a `floor` item when the plan sets a floor: a size named for the floor or bottom and its thickness alone (`floor`, `bottom`, `bottom_thickness`, or `base_thickness`) in a plan with a wall size or a shell feature, or a cut, pocket or shell feature whose `depth` is less than the part's height (its overall z, else its largest height size). The item passes when nothing opens through the part's lowest face. Just above that face, horizontal rays cross the part, and air between its walls with no face above or below it is an opening. Air under a raised floor, such as a recessed foot, is not. Holes no wider than the plan's widest hole (its `holes`, or a size such as `hole_diameter`) are the plan's own, such as a planter's drain holes. A miss names the part, the height of its bottom face and where the air runs, such as "open at the bottom: Vase body: the cavity runs through the bottom face at z 0, 75.03 mm across, from (90.5, 127.6, 0.2) to (165.5, 128.5, 0.2)". Before, a vase whose cavity cut ran out through its bottom passed as an open tube. The body read back has `floor`, with `plannedHoleMm` and each part's `faceZ` and `openings` (`from`, `to`, `alongMm` and `acrossMm`). Plans that set no floor get the same items as before.
