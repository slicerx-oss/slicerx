### Changed

- `slicerx_design_edit_step` 1.6.0 takes `tilt` (`toward` x, -x, y or -y, with `degrees` or `by`): what an extrude, a loft or an added part made leans about its foot as the same step (each added part about its own), the steps built on it lean with it, a pattern made of it leans with it, and a later size or move of the step keeps the lean. A lean that would run it or a step it carries into another step's shape, or lift it off what it stands on, is refused with that step by name, and so is one that a cut, a band of paint or a rounded edge on its top cannot follow.
- `slicerx_design_steps` 1.4.0 gives `leans` (degrees and the way) on a step that leans.
- `slicerx_design_check` 1.18.0 checks loose parts: two parts closer than half the nozzle without touching fail, named with the gap and where it is. A plan whose pieces move or print together (print-in-place pieces, a planned gap) and a part with a thread keep their gaps for the fit check.
- `slicerx_geom_build` 1.4.0, and `slicerx_geom_extrude` 1.12.0, `slicerx_geom_revolve` 1.10.0, `slicerx_geom_loft` 1.8.0 and `slicerx_geom_sweep` 1.11.0 making a new body, seat a new part that lands closer to another part than half the nozzle without touching: it moves along that gap onto the other part, as the same step, and the answer says so. The part is named and left where it is when its gaps point different ways, when the move would be larger than that gap, run it into a third part or leave a gap, or when the step added several parts at once. Pieces that move are never seated.
- `slicerx_geom_mirror` 1.4.0 and `slicerx_geom_array` 1.6.0 do the same for the copies they make on a part: when every copy's gap is the source's, mirrored or turned as the copy is, the one step that made the part moves along the source's gap and the copies follow; otherwise the part is named. Separate copies are named, not moved.
