### Changed

- The tools that measure or change meshes with the server's own geometry engine (mesh analysis, repair, hollow, cut and the rest) give each engine call a time limit by kind of step: 30 s for measures, picks and previews, 3 min for building and joining solids, 5 min for work over a whole mesh (import, repair, simplify, hollow, cut, orient). A call past its limit is stopped and the tool fails with the step's plain name and what to try, such as "sx-geom array: The step "Array" ran past its 3 min limit and was stopped; try a smaller version: fewer copies or a simpler part" or "sx-geom repair: The step "Repair" ran past its 5 min limit and was stopped; try a smaller version: a coarser mesh". A quick step says it took too long and to try again. The stopped call is not run again. In the same turn, mimir does not repeat a stopped modeling or mesh step with the same input on the same design until a geometry change to that design goes through (a modeling step, an edit, an undo or a redo); a save, a setting, a read or a change to another design does not count. The design is the one the step names, by id or by a name an answer in the turn ties to one id, before or after the stop, else the part mimir started last in the run. A step on a mesh model file is not repeated with the same input at all in that turn, since no step changes the file. A quick step may be asked for again. Before, every call had 5 minutes, and one stopped there failed with "returned no JSON".
