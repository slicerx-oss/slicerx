### Changed

- `slicerx_geom_sweep` 1.7.0 (past 1.6.0, the profile with holes) takes `end_profile`: one loop on the profile's own plane (`loops`, and `open: true` for an open chain as the profile has), with no frame of its own. The section turns from `profile` at the start into it by the end, by length along the path with the same `ease`, before `scale` and `twist_deg`. The two are matched point for point as a loft matches its profiles. With `design` it is kept on the step as `endProfile`, so the history shows "Sweep, changing shape" and a saved 3MF opens with it. An end loop that is open where the profile is closed (or the other way round) or that has two loops is refused with `invalid_input` naming `endProfile`, and one with a frame is refused naming `end_profile.frame`. A profile with holes takes no `end_profile` yet: it is refused with `invalid_input` naming `endProfile`, which says to sweep the outside with its end profile and cut the bore with a sweep of its own. Its description now ends "scale, twist_deg and end_profile taper, turn and reshape it", and `end_profile` is described as "Loop it ends as".
