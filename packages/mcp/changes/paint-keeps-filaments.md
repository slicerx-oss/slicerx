### Changed

- `slicerx_project_add_filament` 1.1.0 (from 1.0.0), in the mimir tab: a color the project already has in that material, or one under 40 apart from it (of 441, RGB distance), is refused with the filament to use, such as "Filament 2 is already PLA #1a1a1f, close to #1c1c1e: paint or print with filament 2. Nothing was added." The description says to add only a color something prints or paints in.
- `slicerx_project_set_filament` 1.1.0 (from 1.0.0), in the mimir tab: a type other than the filament's own is refused and nothing changes, such as "Filament 3 stays PETG: a filament's material is not changed. For PLA, add a filament with project.add_filament. Nothing changed." Its own type named again, and a new color, are set as before.
- `slicerx_printer_queue` 1.3.0 (from 1.2.0): with no `slotMap`, the external spool takes a filament only when its color is more than 40 closer than every AMS slot of that material, so a white filament prints from an off-white AMS slot before a pure white external spool.
