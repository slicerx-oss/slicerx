### Fixed

- `slicerx_design_plan` 1.14.0 (from 1.13.0), in the mimir tab, and `slicerx_design_put_plan` 1.8.0 (from 1.7.0) refuse an amend that changes a field an amend does not keep, naming the field: a planned feature's `on`, a row of holes' `recess`, `printInPlace` and, in the mimir tab, the plan's `detail`, `curved`, `flat`, `proportions`, `repeats`, `colors`, `scale`, `real` and `tiers`. Before, an amend that changed one of them along with a size was taken and reported as changed while the field kept its approved value: an amend that moved Funnel array's `on` to another feature and its spacing from 21 mm to 22 mm answered "Plan changed (revision 2): Funnel array spacing 21 mm to 22 mm" and kept the old `on`. The refusal reads, for example, `features: an amend changes sizes only; Funnel array keeps on: "Funnel"`, and the plan stays as approved, with no amend used. A feature restated without its `on`, or a row without its `recess`, is refused the same way; a plan field left out of the amend keeps its approved value, and a scale given as 1:1250 is the same as 1250. A change the person asked for (amends with asked) is held to the same rule. Assumptions and questions stay as approved, as before, and an amend that changes sizes only is taken as before.
