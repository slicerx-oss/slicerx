### Changed

- `slicerx_paint_preview` 1.5.0 (from 1.4.0): a height band on an exact design goes on whole faces. A face the band's edge crosses is split there first, and its shape stays the same. The preview is the step's own replay, and each part's row gains `faces`: the faces the band paints, each with its key, state and area. The band then lasts through the design's later exact steps. An engine that cannot split faces refuses the band on an exact design, as before.
- `slicerx_paint_apply` 1.4.0 (from 1.3.0) and `slicerx_paint_regions` 1.2.0 (from 1.1.0): on an exact design, each part's read-back gains `faces`, every face painted whole with its key, state and area in mm2.
- `slicerx_paint_regions` 1.2.0 also lists `bare` on a part: each face left unpainted though it was painted, with its key, area and the reason. Today that is a face a later step joined from pieces painted differently. A band whose faces could not be split cleanly is refused in one sentence, and the error's `details.cause` (`clash` or `unnamed`) says which way it failed.
- `slicerx_paint_preview` 1.5.0 also paints a direction (faces up, for one) on an exact design face by face. A face it reaches is painted whole when the face's own normal is within the angle. A curved face that only partly faces that way stays bare, so the design stays exact. The preview is the step's own replay here too, and its rows name the faces painted.
