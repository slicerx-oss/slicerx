### Changed

- `slicerx_design_check` 1.20.0 (from 1.19.0): for a display or fine plan, a new item, "stands on", holds each stacked part to what the plan says it stands on, named in `on` or given by a tier list. Its base must sit within 0.2 mm of its support's top, and its planned height must show above that top within 1.35 times; with no height planned, the base alone is held. A failure says which way it is off, for example "Funnel 1 stands on Upper deckhouse, but its base is 6.65 mm below its top, so 8.35 mm of it shows of a planned 15 mm; build it from the top of Upper deckhouse". Heights are read from the design's own steps: extrudes that rise straight up, lofts, revolves about an upright axis and step patterns. A pair is skipped when either side was built by a step that gives only a bound (a sweep), or by one whose height is not read (an array of a whole part, a boolean, a mesh step). A plan with no such pair gets no item.
