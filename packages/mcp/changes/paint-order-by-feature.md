### Changed

- `slicerx_paint_preview` 1.8.0 (from 1.7.0): its `target` says that painting a body by feature also colors its top faces, and gives the order for a body of its own color: by feature first, then its top in its own color with direction "top" at a small angle (about 5 degrees), then any band across features by height last.
- `slicerx_app_paint_preview` 1.4.0 (from 1.3.0): its `target` says the same, as `slicerx_paint_preview` does.
