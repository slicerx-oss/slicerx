### Changed

- `slicerx_design_put_plan` 1.5.0 takes `openFaces` in the plan, optional: the faces of a piece meant to be open, such as `[{ "piece": "Pen cup", "faces": ["top"] }]` for a cup open at the top. Each entry has `piece` (the part's own name, or a piece `printInPlace` names) and `faces`, one or more of `top`, `bottom`, `front`, `back`, `left` and `right` as the piece prints. The plan keeps them for later checks; `slicerx_design_check` does not read them yet. The result gives `openFaces` back and the summary names each, such as "Pen cup open at the top". The tool refuses a face it does not know ("\"rim\" is no face"), a face or piece named twice, all six faces of a piece, an empty list or an entry with no faces, and a piece the plan does not have ("openFaces: Lid is no piece of this plan; name the part (Pen cup)"), and keeps nothing. An amend keeps the plan's `openFaces` as they are. A plan without `openFaces` is kept and answered as before.
