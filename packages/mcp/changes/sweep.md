### Added

- `slicerx_geom_sweep` 1.0.0: a solid swept from one closed sketch profile (`profile`, `frame` and `loops` as for `slicerx_geom_revolve`) along an open path (`path`: one open chain of lines and arcs on a plane as `frame` and `loops`, or an open polyline in space as `points`), as a new body (`operation` `new`), joined to a target or cut from it. The profile keeps its place and turn relative to the path and does not twist about it, with straight sides between path points and flat ends square to the path. A straight path sweeps exactly the extrusion. Refused with a plain sentence: a profile that is open, has holes or is more than one loop; a path that is closed, crosses itself or doubles back; a profile whose plane is within 1 degree of parallel to the path's start; a bend tighter than the profile can follow; and a body that runs into itself where the path comes back near itself. With `design` it adds a `sketch.sweep` step (named "Sweep", or "Sweep cut") that replays and saves in the 3MF like every other step, and takes finished sketch ids as `profile_sketch` and `path_sketch`; without one it writes an STL with volume, bounds and whether it is watertight. App link method `geom.sweep` (edit class).
