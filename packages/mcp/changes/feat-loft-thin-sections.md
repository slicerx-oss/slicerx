### Changed

- `slicerx_geom_loft` 1.11.0 (from 1.10.0) names a section under two nozzle lines across, for a new body or a join on a design: the summary ends with a note for the thinnest such section, such as "Section 3 of 3 is 0.46 mm across at its narrowest, under the 0.8 mm a 0.4 mm nozzle prints (two lines). Widen it there, or leave it if another body covers that edge.", and the answer gains `thinSections`, each section's place, the section count and its thinnest in mm, thinnest first. The loft is never refused for it. A section is read by its outer outline in its own plane; a pointed keel or nose (two sides meeting within two nozzle lines) is a tip, not a thin wall. A cut, a thickened loft and an open chain get no note. Replaying a design gives no note, so saved designs load as before.
- `slicerx_design_edit_step` 1.7.0 (from 1.6.2) gives the same note and `thinSections` after a `section` edit that leaves a section under two nozzle lines across. The edit is kept either way.
- `slicerx_design_plan` 1.13.0 (from 1.12.0), in the mimir tab: the description asks for each section of a curved body to be at least two nozzle widths across at every point, the end ones too, so a bow or a nose ends on a narrow rounded section, not a knife edge.
