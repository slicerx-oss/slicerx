### Changed

- `slicerx_design_plan` 1.18.0, in the mimir tab: `flat` takes each flat-sided main body with its levels, as `{feature, levels}` with its levels from the bottom up (at least one; a single block lists only itself), in place of a list of names with a separate `tiers` map. A body given this way always states its levels, so the "give the levels of" refusal cannot happen, and a body named by a word that is no planned feature, over levels that all are, is read as the levels of one body keyed by its lowest, so the refusal for a tier list keyed by a group name cannot happen either. The older form, `flat` as names with `tiers`, is still taken, with its refusals as before, and the card keeps a plan in that form, so saved plans read back as they did. The help a display plan's refusals and the tool's description carry says a stepped body is given by its own feature with its levels in order.
