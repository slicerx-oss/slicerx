### Fixed

- `slicerx_geom_push_pull` 1.5.1 (from 1.5.0) pushes a flat top down along a round wall, such as the top of a cylinder or of a slot with round ends. Before, the push could be refused with "the result is unsafe at mesh storage precision (intersecting faces)" when the top was a hair off flat, as a kernel's tessellation leaves a round top, or when the wall had points between its top and bottom rims. A push down now also takes the wall's bulge past the top's outline with it, so no thin flap of the wall is left standing over the new top. `slicerx_geom_mirror` 1.7.1 (from 1.7.0) and `slicerx_geom_array` 1.10.1 (from 1.10.0), which repeat a push step, and `slicerx_design_edit_step` 1.9.1 (from 1.9.0), which runs a push step again with a new distance, give the same push. A push on a box or a bar answers as before.
