That covers anything from an arc that dips a tenth of a millimeter under the body's lowest line to a part built centered on z 0. The part is only ever raised, so a part built standing above the bed stays where it is. The lift moves the design as a whole, like the move onto the bed: points given and read back stay in the frame the design was built in, and `placedMm` gets a third number, the lift in mm. The note on that call says how far it went, such as "The part was under the bed, so it was lifted 10 mm onto it as a whole." or "The part was off the bed, so it moved to the plate by (128, 128) mm and was lifted 28 mm as a whole." Before, such a part stayed under the bed, and each later step said it was off the bed and left it there.
- A project saved with a part under the bed opens with the part where it was saved, still marked off the bed in the object list, and its next edit lifts it. A saved project keeps the lift with the design's other landing moves; a project saved before lifts opens with no lift.
