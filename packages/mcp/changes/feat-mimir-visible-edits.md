### Changed

- `slicerx_design_plan` 1.9.0 (from 1.8.0), in the mimir tab, takes `personSize`. A display or fine plan without it is planned to fill about 85% of the printer's usable bed: its longest side, held to the other side and the height, from the printer profile's printable area and height, or 256 mm when no printer is chosen. A plan sized otherwise is refused with the size to plan. Each level of a stepped body is at least 3 mm tall. The description says up front that bodies are named by feature, that a loft's section count is a size named sections, and that an amend comes only once the part is built. A plan waits for the person's Go only when its sizes must fit something (a sheet with `fits` in the run) or its pieces print assembled and move; any other plan, at its default size, shows on its card and is approved as shown at once, with `auto: true` in the answer. Amends follow the same rule.
- `slicerx_design_check` 1.17.0 (from 1.16.0): a display or fine part that passes says to give its size in the reply and offer a resize as a follow-up.
