### Changed

- `slicerx_app_sheet_ask` 1.1.0 takes `design`, the id of a design already built that the sheet changes; the sheet keeps it, and mimir's own sheet draws that design as built with each row's `at` in its coordinates. A `design` that is not on the plate is refused with `not_found`. `part` takes a revolve (`kind` `revolve`: a profile, its `axis` in the profile's plane and an `angle`), a sweep (`kind` `sweep`: a round `profile` of diameter `d` with an optional bore `hole`, or an outline, along a `path` whose corners bend on radius `round`) and a loft (`kind` `loft`: `sections` at offsets along the plane's third axis, round or outlines) beside extruded outlines, so a pipe bend, a round stem or a vase is drawn as it will be built. Its description says how to give `part`, `at` and `design`. In mimir's sheet every row of a sheet of several sizes needs `at`, so each row gets its callout. Each `part` solid is one object whose `kind` (`extrude` when left out) says which of its fields it needs, and the description gives a short example of each kind. A solid or a row's `at` that is missing a field is refused with `invalid_input` naming the part or row and the shape the field takes, such as `row 2 (Bore) needs at: {"a": [x, y, z], "b": [x, y, z], "off": [x, y, z]} in mm`. Fields of another kind are dropped.
- `slicerx_mimir_dismiss` 1.1.0 also sets aside a size sheet mimir waits on when `sheetId` names it, as the person typing to mimir while it waits does: mimir reads `said` in its place, with any sizes the person had typed on it, and builds nothing from it; with no `said` the sheet is canceled. It never confirms a sheet. Takes `sheetId` from `waiting`, refused with `not_found` when that sheet no longer waits, and answers with `sheetId` and `setAside: true` (or `canceled: true`). A call without `sheetId` never touches a sheet: with no question waiting it is still refused with `not_found`, and while a size sheet waits the refusal names that sheet and asks for its `sheetId`.
- `slicerx_mimir_session` 1.2.0 shows a waiting size sheet in `waiting` (`sheetId`, `title` and its `sizes`), and `stats.sheetsSetAside` counts the sheets set aside by words, apart from canceled ones.
