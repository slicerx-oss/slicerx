### Changed

- `slicerx_design_plan` 1.18.0 (from 1.17.2), in the mimir tab: an amend sends only what changes. It takes `changes`, each size to change as `{feature, size, to}` (a planned feature's name and one of its sizes, `overall` with x, y or z, or a row of holes' name with `diameter`), and `add`, features to add after the planned ones. With `amends`, `part`, `units`, `features`, `assumptions` and `questions` may be left out and are the approved plan's, and the overall size, rows of holes and open faces left out are too, for a build's amend as well as a change the person asked for; the changes apply onto that plan. A change that names no planned size is refused, naming it and what the plan has. A plan field an amend does not keep (`printInPlace`, `detail`, `curved`, `flat`, `proportions`, `repeats`, `colors`, `scale`, `real`, `tiers`, and the part's name) sent otherwise than the plan has it is no longer refused: the plan keeps it as approved, the sizes still change, and the result names it in `ignored` and in its summary. A whole plan sent with `amends`, as before, still works, with `changes` on top of it when both are given. `changes` and `add` without `amends` are refused. A first plan still needs its part, units, features, assumptions and questions.
