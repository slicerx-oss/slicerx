### Changed

- `slicerx_geom_extrude` 1.5.0, `slicerx_geom_revolve` 1.5.0, `slicerx_geom_loft` 1.3.0, `slicerx_geom_sweep` 1.3.0, `slicerx_geom_push_pull` 1.4.0, `slicerx_geom_boolean` 1.3.0, `slicerx_geom_fillet` 1.3.0, `slicerx_geom_chamfer` 1.3.0, `slicerx_geom_shell` 1.3.0, `slicerx_geom_hole` 1.3.0, `slicerx_geom_cut` 1.3.0, `slicerx_geom_split` 1.3.0, `slicerx_geom_mirror` 1.2.0, `slicerx_geom_array` 1.3.0, `slicerx_geom_repair` 1.2.0, `slicerx_geom_hollow` 1.2.0, `slicerx_geom_emboss` 1.2.0, `slicerx_geom_build` 1.2.0, `slicerx_geom_subtract` 1.2.0, `slicerx_geom_faces` 1.3.0, `slicerx_geom_face_pick` 1.3.0, `slicerx_geom_edge_pick` 1.2.0, `slicerx_geom_info` 1.3.0, `slicerx_geom_measure` 1.2.0, `slicerx_design_sketch_project` 1.1.0, `slicerx_design_list` 1.1.0, `slicerx_design_steps` 1.2.0, `slicerx_design_edit_step` 1.2.0, `slicerx_design_undo` 1.1.0 and `slicerx_design_redo` 1.1.0 on the app's plate: when a design is moved onto the bed (a first body built off it, or an edit that takes it off), it moves as a whole and keeps its own coordinates. Points given and read back, and the bounds in the design summary, stay in the frame the design was built in, so a step given after the move still meets the body. Before, the design's coordinates became the plate's, and such a step missed it with "The shape no longer reaches the part". The design summary has `placedMm`, how far those moves took it on the plate; the plate tools report the object's transform, which is the design's frame moved by `placedMm`. The note on the call that moved it says it moved as a whole and that its coordinates stay the design's own. Where the plan feature tags (mimir-build-flow.md) changed the same tool, the version here is past both.
- `slicerx_design_new` 1.1.0 and `slicerx_design_open` 1.3.0 return the design summary with `placedMm` and with bounds in the design's own frame. A design started from a model built off the bed is moved onto it as a whole, with the same note as the modeling tools, and a project saved after a design was moved onto the bed opens with that move, so the design's points stay in the frame it was built in.
- The same tools, when a design's first body has no free spot on the bed: the other designs moved onto the bed before, that the person has not moved since, are moved over, each as a whole, to make room, and the note names each one with how far it moved. `slicerx_design_undo` of the step that landed the new design moves them back and leaves the new design where it was built, and `slicerx_design_redo` lands it again. When something on the plate moves, is locked or unlocked, or an object is added or removed, while the room is worked out, it is worked out again, so the new design never lands on a design the person just moved or an object just put down, and a design the person just locked is never moved. Before, the new body stayed where it was built, off the bed.
- `slicerx_design_sketch` 1.3.0, `slicerx_design_sketch_svg` 1.1.0 and `slicerx_design_trace_image` 1.1.0 take `design`: the sketch's frame, and the default center on the bed, are in that design's coordinates, and the sketch moves with that design (a landing, room made for another design, a person's drag), also after a step on it is undone or while the design waits for its body. Without `design`, the frame is the plate's while no design was moved onto the bed; after that the sketch is drawn for the only design there is, with the default center on the middle of the bed as the plate shows it, or refused with `invalid_input` while there are others (a dry run of `slicerx_design_trace_image` still reports). A finished sketch given as `sketch`, `sketches`, `profile_sketch` or `path_sketch` comes in the coordinates of the design it is used on, where it stands now: drawn for no design, it moves with the body of the first step drawn on it, and is where it was drawn once no step uses it. Drawing on it leaves the sketch, and every step on it, where they were, whatever is undone, redone or deleted later, and a step the person adds on it in Model is kept the same way.
- `slicerx_design_sketch_project` projects onto the sketch where it stands now, after the design that owns it moved, so the curves meet the body they came from and a step drawn from them lands there.
- A saved project keeps how far each design was moved onto the bed, and the design each sketch was drawn for, so after it opens again the design keeps its coordinates and its sketches. A project saved before sketches moved with their designs opens with each sketch where the app that saved it showed it: from an object history file (history version 1 or 2), moved with the body of the step it came from; from an older design file, where it was stored. From then on it moves with that body.
