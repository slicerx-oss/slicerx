- `slicerx_geom_extrude` 1.4.0, `slicerx_geom_revolve` 1.4.0, `slicerx_geom_loft` 1.2.0, `slicerx_geom_sweep` 1.2.0, `slicerx_geom_push_pull` 1.3.0, `slicerx_geom_boolean` 1.2.0, `slicerx_geom_fillet` 1.2.0, `slicerx_geom_chamfer` 1.2.0, `slicerx_geom_shell` 1.2.0, `slicerx_geom_hole` 1.2.0, `slicerx_geom_cut` 1.2.0, `slicerx_geom_split` 1.2.0, `slicerx_geom_mirror` 1.1.0, `slicerx_geom_array` 1.2.0, `slicerx_geom_repair` 1.1.0, `slicerx_geom_hollow` 1.1.0, `slicerx_geom_emboss` 1.1.0, `slicerx_geom_build` 1.1.0, `slicerx_geom_subtract` 1.1.0, `slicerx_geom_faces` 1.3.0, `slicerx_geom_face_pick` 1.3.0, `slicerx_geom_edge_pick` 1.2.0, `slicerx_geom_info` 1.3.0, `slicerx_geom_measure` 1.2.0, `slicerx_design_sketch_project` 1.1.0, `slicerx_paint_preview` 1.1.0, `slicerx_paint_apply` 1.1.0, `slicerx_paint_regions` 1.1.0, `slicerx_design_new` 1.1.0, `slicerx_design_open` 1.3.0, `slicerx_design_list` 1.1.0, `slicerx_design_steps` 1.1.0, `slicerx_design_edit_step` 1.1.0, `slicerx_design_undo` 1.1.0, `slicerx_design_redo` 1.1.0 and `slicerx_design_save` 1.1.0 on the app's plate: when a design is moved onto the bed (a first body built off it, or an edit that takes it off), it moves as a whole and keeps its own coordinates. Points given and read back stay in the frame the design was built in, so a step given after the move still meets the body. Before, the design's coordinates became the plate's, and such a step missed it with "The shape no longer reaches the part". The design summary has `placedMm`, how far those moves took it on the plate; the plate tools report the object's transform, which is the design's frame moved by `placedMm`. The note on the call that moved it says it moved as a whole and that its coordinates stay the design's own.
- The same tools, when a design's first body has no free spot on the bed: the other designs moved onto the bed before are moved over, each as a whole, to make room, and the note names each one with how far it moved. Before, the new body stayed where it was built, off the bed.
- `slicerx_design_sketch` 1.3.0 and `slicerx_design_sketch_svg` 1.1.0 take `design`: the sketch's frame, and the SVG's default center, are in that design's coordinates, and the sketch is kept in the plate's. A finished sketch given as `sketch`, `sketches`, `profile_sketch` or `path_sketch` comes in the coordinates of the design it is used on. Drawing on it leaves the sketch, and a person's features on it, where they were.
- A saved project keeps how far each design was moved onto the bed, so after it opens again the design keeps its coordinates.
