### Changed

- `slicerx_design_list` 1.2.0 (from 1.1.0): each design carries `forms`, one short line per part saying how it was made and how it looks: what made it (a box, a cylinder, a turned outline, a loft of N sections), what joined it, whether any edge is rounded or beveled, "plain block" for a part of boxes and cylinders with every edge sharp, and where it sits. Past 16 parts the rest are counted in `formsMore`. Every design tool answer that reads a design back carries the same lines.
