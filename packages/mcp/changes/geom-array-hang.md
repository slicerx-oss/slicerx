### Fixed

- `slicerx_geom_array` 1.1.1 joins a merged circular array much faster when the part has a round on the array's own axis and the copies go all the way round, turned with the part. A 100 sided cylinder, a tube, or a hub with one tooth and a bore, in 12 copies, now joins in well under a second instead of running for minutes. The round's facets are lined up with the copies, so it can come back with a few more sides (100 become 108 for 12 copies). A plain round and a bore keep their corners on their circle, and a round joined to other shapes grows by at most 0.01 mm. A round with something else inside it (a pin in a bore, a recess, an engraving), a partial sweep, or copies that are not turned is joined as before and can still take long. A merged result that would not store is now refused with "the result is unsafe at mesh storage precision", where before it came back and failed later. A merged join stops with "this join is taking too long to finish; try fewer copies or join them in smaller groups" once it has handed the engine more than about 20 million triangles of work. A merged join that ends with crossing faces gets one pass through the robust engine and can report engine `robust`. Other joins, such as `slicerx_geom_boolean`, are unchanged.
