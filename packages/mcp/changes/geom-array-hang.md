### Fixed

- `slicerx_geom_array` 1.1.1 merges a circular array much faster when the part has a round, or a plain bore, on the array's own axis and the copies go all the way round, turned with the part: a 100 sided cylinder, a tube or a bored hub with a tooth in 12 copies now takes well under a second instead of minutes. Such a round can come back with a few more sides (100 become 108 for 12 copies) and grow by at most 0.01 mm. Other parts, and partial or unturned arrays, merge as before. A merged result that would not store is now refused with "the merged copies are unsafe at mesh storage precision". A merge that ends with crossing faces gets one robust pass and can report engine `robust`. `slicerx_geom_boolean` is unchanged.
