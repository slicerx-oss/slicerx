### Changed

- `slicerx_design_check` 1.5.0 (past 1.4.0, the plan kept in a saved project) checks the hook of a plan with a hook and a lip (a hook among its features and a lip in their names, kinds or sizes): the hook on the part's upright stem must end in a tip that rises at least 3 mm above the hook's lowest point toward its end, as item `hook lip`. A tip that does not rise, or no hook reaching out past the stem, fails it. A part with no upright round stem to read the hook from has no such item.
