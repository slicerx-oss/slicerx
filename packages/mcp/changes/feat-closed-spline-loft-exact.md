### Changed

- `slicerx_geom_loft` 1.15.0 (from 1.14.0) makes a new body lofted through profiles that are each one closed spline as an exact solid, where the engine makes exact solids. Before, it was made as a mesh, and the answer ended with "Profile 1 is a closed spline, which has no corners to match its pieces between." Now it answers `exact: true` with no note. Each closed spline is one piece all the way around, as a whole circle is: places along it go by its length, and each profile starts where it twists least from the profile before, so a profile drawn the other way around or from another point lines up with its neighbors. A closed spline beside a profile whose splines meet at corners is cut where those corners land on it, and each piece is made spline to spline. A closed spline beside lines, arcs or a circle is still made as a mesh, and the note now reads "A spline is matched only to a spline, and profile 2 has none where profile 1 has one." The mesh such a loft gives, when it is made as a mesh, is unchanged.
