### Added

- Sketch loops take an ellipse: `{"type": "ellipse", "center": [u, v], "widthMm": w, "heightMm": h, "angleDeg": a}`, its full axis lengths with the width axis turned `angleDeg` (default 0) counterclockwise from `u`. `slicerx_geom_sketch_check` 1.2.0 (from 1.1.0), `slicerx_geom_sketch_edit` 1.2.0 (from 1.1.0), `slicerx_geom_extrude` 1.10.0 (from 1.9.0, as `shape` `{"type": "sketch", "loops": [...]}`), `slicerx_geom_revolve` 1.9.0 (from 1.8.0), `slicerx_geom_loft` 1.7.0 (from 1.6.0), `slicerx_geom_sweep` 1.10.0 (from 1.9.0, as a profile) and `slicerx_design_sketch` 1.4.0 (from 1.3.0) all accept it. A design keeps it as given and replays it as a true curve. `slicerx_geom_sketch_check` reads it back like any loop: its `areaMm2` and `lengthMm` (perimeter) per loop. A missing or zero size is refused with the field named. `slicerx_geom_sketch_edit` mirrors and patterns an ellipse whole, its `angleDeg` turned with the copy; intersect, trim and extend refuse a sketch with one.
- `slicerx_geom_extrude` 1.10.0 takes the shape `{"type": "ellipse", "widthMm": w, "heightMm": h}`, centered on `placement.center` with its width along `u` before `placement.rotationDeg` turns it. It takes every extrude option, a taper included.

### Changed

- `slicerx_geom_edge_pick` 1.5.0 (from 1.4.0), `slicerx_geom_fillet` 1.5.0 (from 1.4.1) and `slicerx_geom_chamfer` 1.5.0 (from 1.4.1) refuse an edge that goes around an ellipse, such as the rim of an extruded ellipse. Its loop shows every side unsupported and no chain or loop size. Before, they rounded or cut one short facet of the rim on its own. `slicerx_geom_edge_pick` now reports such an edge with `supported: false` and the reason "The edge goes around an ellipse. Fillet and chamfer work on straight edges and round rims, not on ellipses yet." The fillet and chamfer tools refuse it with `invalid_input` and "edges: edge 1: the edge goes around an ellipse...", and nothing changes.
