### Added

- `slicerx_printer_home` 1.0.0 homes every axis of a printer (G28), as the Home button on the app's Move tab does. A person approves it on a card that says the nozzle moves down to the plate. The card is its own action, `printer.home` with work kind `home`, never a raw G-code line, and the hub checks the printer and the plate again when the person approves: it homes only while the printer is idle or finished, and only when the person says on the card that the plate is clear (or the hub already knows it is), and otherwise fails with `bad_state` or `bed_check`. A phone can only deny it. It is refused before any card unless the printer is idle or finished.
- `slicerx_printer_status` and `slicerx_printer_list` (detail `full`) return `firmware`, the version a Bambu Lab printer reports, and `live.units[].climate`, what each filament unit's sensors read: `humidityPct`, `humidityLevel` (1 dry to 5 wet, when the unit gives no percentage), `tempC`, `drying` (only on units that can dry) and `dryMinutesLeft`. They are read only; no tool sets a unit's dryer.
