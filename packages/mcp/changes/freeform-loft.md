### Changed

- `slicerx_geom_loft` 1.1.0: `smooth` runs the sides along a smooth curve through the profiles (centripetal Catmull-Rom through matching points, flattened to 0.005 mm) instead of straight lines from each profile to the next, and `closed` joins the last profile back to the first as a ring with no ends (3 or more profiles). A profile may set `open` to be one open chain (its `points` a polyline), which makes a surface, so the loft then needs `thicken` (`thicknessMm`, and `side`: `outward`, the default, `inward` or `center`) to make it a solid shell with walls along its open edges. `thicken` also works on closed profiles, making the sides a shell with no ends. A profile may mark `landmarks`, points on it that match the same points on every other profile in order (a hull station's keel, bilge, waterline and sheer): each run between two of them is spread over the same number of points on every profile. The step is named "Smooth loft", "Loft ring" and with the shell's thickness, and replays from the 3MF. New refusals, each one sentence: open and closed profiles mixed, an open loft without `thicken`, a closed loft of fewer than 3 profiles, landmarks off their profile, out of order, in different numbers or running opposite ways, a body or shell that runs into itself, and a shell thicker than the surface's tightest bend allows on that side (it says the most that bend takes).
- `slicerx_geom_sweep` 1.1.0: `profile.open` and `thicken`, as for the loft.
