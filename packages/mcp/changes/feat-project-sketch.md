### Added

- `slicerx_design_sketch_project` 1.0.0 projects model geometry onto a finished sketch's plane as sketch curves: picked edges (`kind` `edges`, `edges` from `slicerx_geom_edge_pick`, chained where they meet), a face's outline with its holes (`kind` `face`, `face` as for `slicerx_geom_push_pull`) or a body's whole outline seen along the sketch normal (`kind` `silhouette`), from the body `design`. `mode` `real` (the default) makes them profile curves for `slicerx_geom_extrude` and `slicerx_geom_revolve`; `reference` keeps them as construction curves that make no profile. An extrude or revolve made from the sketch before a projection never takes it, so projecting never changes an existing solid. With `projection` it switches one's `mode` or, with `remove`, takes it out with its curves. The curves follow their source when the design changes earlier in the timeline, and the bodies drawn on the sketch rebuild; a source that is gone keeps its curves and is reported as `lost`. Returns every projection of the sketch with its `kind`, `body`, `mode`, `loops` and `lost`, and how many bodies rebuilt. Runs on the project open in the app; app link method `design.sketch_project` (edit class).
