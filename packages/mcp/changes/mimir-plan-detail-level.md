### Changed

- `slicerx_design_plan` 1.7.0 (from 1.6.0), in the mimir tab, takes `detail` (basic, display or fine), and with display or fine, `curved` and `flat` (the features that build each main body, by whether the real thing is curved or flat-sided), `proportions` (a lookup or a stated source), `repeats` (the real object's repeated details) `colors` (each feature's color as the real thing is painted, by feature name, such as `{"Lower body": "red"}`), `scale` (N of 1:N, as a number, as digits such as "1200", or as "1:N"), `real` (the real sizes the source gives in mm at full size, each as `{feature, size, mm}`) and `tiers` (each flat-sided main body's levels from the bottom up, by body name). Display is meant as the default for a real object named without sizes. A display or fine plan is refused, before any card shows, when it builds a curved body without a loft, revolve or sweep, lofts or sweeps it through fewer than 5 sections (8 at fine), names no body, leaves out its proportions or repeats, lists repeated details with no array or pattern feature, gives no `colors`, gives one feature two colors such as "red and black" (split it at its color line instead), leaves a main body's color out, gives real sizes with no scale or with no main body among them, plans a size more than 1.35 times off what its real size gives at scale (either way), plans a part wider or longer than the largest main body or taller than the whole model, leaves out a flat-sided main body's `tiers` or names a level that is not a feature, or has fewer features than its level asks: 8 at display and 12 at fine, 2 more when the overall's longest side is 4 or more times its next, and one more for each repeated detail past three; the refusal says what to plan instead and how such a model is built. A flat-sided body passes with an extrude. The card shows the level in plain words and the person can change it before Go; a changed level comes back in `edits`. A plan with no `detail` and a basic one are held to none of this. `slicerx_design_put_plan` does not take these fields.
- `slicerx_design_check` 1.15.0 (from 1.14.0) reads the minimum wall away from sharp edges and points. A reading under twice the nozzle between two faces that meet within two nozzle widths of where it was taken, the taper of a rim, a knife edge or a nose, is left out of the minimum wall: the item passes on the thinnest wall elsewhere and carries `warning`, with how thin the taper reads and where. A real thin wall away from any such edge still fails. A part split flat at a flared color line read 0.04 mm at its rim and failed though its body was 0.9 mm. Any passed item can now carry `warning`, and the summary lists the warnings. For a display or fine plan, each curved body built by a loft gets a `curved bodies` item: a body whose sections grow or shrink in one direction across it while their top and bottom in the other stay put (by 3 percent of its largest cross size) fails, so a body whose sections all stand from the same bottom to the same top, only narrower toward the ends, fails and says which size never changes. Sections that change both ways pass, and so does one outline carried along at its size (a raked tube). A display or fine plan's overall size passes within 1 mm or 5 percent, whichever is larger, with `warning` naming the miss when it is past the tight 0.2 mm or 1 percent, so a smooth loft's small bulge needs no plan change.
- `slicerx_app_sheet_ask` 1.3.0 (from 1.2.0) takes a row's `at` with no `off`: the drawing moves its callout off the part itself. A count row (kind `count`, or a key that reads as one) needs no `at` on a sheet of several sizes; it shows in the list with no callout. A row with `source` `lookup` takes `sourceTitle` without `sourceUrl`, for a lookup that gave no link, and shows the source with no link; one with neither is still refused. `part` says in its description that a sheet of several sizes needs it unless `design` names a built part.
- `slicerx_design_put_plan` 1.6.0 (from 1.5.0) takes `recess: true` on a row of `holes`, for a blind recess that does not go through; `slicerx_design_check` reads the part's blind openings on any face, flat or curved (a round dent, a slit cut into a lofted side), with each one's width, length and depth, and holds a recess row to them by count and size: a row with fewer at its size fails and says what was read. A recess row is never met by a through hole, and a hole a recess row took is not listed as an extra hole. The body facts carry `recesses` when the plan has a recess row. `slicerx_design_plan` takes it too.
- `slicerx_design_check` meets a planned color band by paint: a feature named or of a kind that is a band or a stripe (or of a paint kind), with its color in the plan's `colors` and its heights in its sizes (from and to, an edge and its height, or below or above a height), needs no step that shapes it when the part's paint reads back a region of that color running between those heights, each end within 1 mm or a fifth of the band's height. The feature count says where it was painted. A band painted in another color, at another height, in a filament whose color is not known, or not at all, is held to a step as before, and the item says what paint was read. In the app, the filament colors are the ones on the rail.
- `slicerx_design_plan` refuses a plan that names its pieces in `printInPlace` and lays them apart (laid or printed apart, or 2 mm or more apart): a lid that prints in place prints on its hinge, open flat on the shared hinge axis with its knuckles between the base's, or closed on the base. Only a lid put on after printing lies apart, 10 mm from the base, and it is then left out of `printInPlace`. A lid opened flat beside its box on the hinge is still taken. `slicerx_design_put_plan` keeps such a plan and says so.
- In the mimir tab, `slicerx_design_check` reads the open project's filament colors, as it already did over the app link, so a painted band is held to them. Before, the tab read every filament's color as unknown, so a band painted where and as planned never counted as built.
