### Changed

- `slicerx_design_plan` 1.6.0 (from 1.5.0), in the mimir tab, takes `detail` (basic, display or fine), and with display or fine, `curved` and `flat` (the features that build each main body, by whether the real thing is curved or flat-sided), `proportions` (a lookup or a stated source), `repeats` (the real object's repeated details) and `colors` (each feature's color as the real thing is painted, as `{feature, color}`). Display is meant as the default for a real object named without sizes. A display or fine plan is refused, before any card shows, when it builds a curved body without a loft, revolve or sweep, lofts or sweeps it through fewer than 5 sections (8 at fine), names no body, leaves out its proportions or repeats, lists repeated details with no array or pattern feature, gives no `colors`, gives one feature two colors (split it at its color line instead), leaves a main body's color out, or has fewer features than its level asks: 8 at display and 12 at fine, 2 more when the overall's longest side is 4 or more times its next, and one more for each repeated detail past three; the refusal says what to plan instead and how such a model is built. A flat-sided body passes with an extrude. The card shows the level in plain words and the person can change it before Go; a changed level comes back in `edits`. A plan with no `detail` and a basic one are held to none of this. `slicerx_design_put_plan` does not take these fields.
- `slicerx_design_check` 1.15.0 (from 1.14.0) reads the minimum wall away from sharp edges and points. A reading under twice the nozzle between two faces that meet within two nozzle widths of where it was taken, the taper of a rim, a knife edge or a bow, is left out of the minimum wall: the item passes on the thinnest wall elsewhere and carries `warning`, with how thin the taper reads and where. A real thin wall away from any such edge still fails. A part split flat at a flared waterline read 0.04 mm at its rim and failed though its body was 0.9 mm. Any passed item can now carry `warning`, and the summary lists the warnings. A part with too many facets to measure the wall in time passes the minimum wall with `warning` that it was not measured. Before, it failed with "not measured (part too dense)". For a display or fine plan, each curved body built by a loft gets a `curved bodies` item: a body whose sections grow or shrink in one direction across it while their top and bottom in the other stay put (by 3 percent of its largest cross size) fails, so a hull whose sections all stand from the same bottom to the same deck, only narrower toward the ends, fails and says which size never changes. Sections that change both ways pass, and so does one outline carried along at its size (a raked funnel). A display or fine plan's overall size passes within 1 mm or 5 percent, whichever is larger, with `warning` naming the miss when it is past the tight 0.2 mm or 1 percent, so a smooth loft's small bulge needs no plan change.
