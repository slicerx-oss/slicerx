### Changed

- `slicerx_design_plan` 1.6.0 (from 1.5.0), in the mimir tab, takes `detail` (basic, display or fine), and with display or fine, `curved` and `flat` (the features that build each main body, by whether the real thing is curved or flat-sided), `proportions` (a lookup or a stated source) and `repeats` (the real object's repeated details). Display is meant as the default for a real object named without sizes. A display or fine plan is refused, before any card shows, when it builds a curved body without a loft, revolve or sweep, lofts or sweeps it through fewer than 5 sections (8 at fine), names no body, leaves out its proportions or repeats, or lists repeated details with no array or pattern feature; the refusal says what to plan instead and how such a model is built. A flat-sided body passes with an extrude. The card shows the level in plain words and the person can change it before Go; a changed level comes back in `edits`. A plan with no `detail` and a basic one are held to none of this. `slicerx_design_put_plan` does not take these fields.
- `slicerx_design_check` 1.15.0 (from 1.14.0) reads the minimum wall away from sharp edges and points. A reading under twice the nozzle between two faces that meet within two nozzle widths of where it was taken, the taper of a rim, a knife edge or a bow, is left out of the minimum wall: the item passes on the thinnest wall elsewhere and carries `warning`, with how thin the taper reads and where. A real thin wall away from any such edge still fails. A part split flat at a flared waterline read 0.04 mm at its rim and failed though its body was 0.9 mm. Any passed item can now carry `warning`, and the summary lists the warnings. A part with too many facets to measure the wall in time passes the minimum wall with `warning` that it was not measured. Before, it failed with "not measured (part too dense)".
