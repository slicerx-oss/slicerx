### Changed

- `slicerx_geom_hole` 2.1.0 (from 2.0.1) takes a `face` point a little off the part, as a point on a curved side often is. When no face is within 0.02 mm of `at`, and the line through `at` along `normal` meets the part within 1 mm on a face turned at most 30 degrees from `normal`, the hole starts where that line meets the part and goes in square to the surface there. The answer ends with where it moved, such as "(40, -11, 10) is 0.228 mm off the surface, so the hole starts where its direction meets the surface, at (40, -10.772, 10)", and `onto` gives `from`, `to` and `mm`. A point farther off is refused with the surface point nearest it and how far it is, such as "at: no face within 0.02 mm of (20, -11, 10); the surface nearest it is at (20.209, -9.623, 10), 1.393 mm away; give a point on the face the hole starts at". On a curved face the cut starts 0.05 mm outside the face (`extent` `twoSides`, `distance2Mm` 0.05), so an exact body keeps only the hole's wall and floor and no sliver face where the cut's end would only touch the face; a hole on a flat face is cut as before. A hole on a curved side cannot yet be changed with `slicerx_geom_hole_edit`: the hole finder reads only holes that start on a flat face, so the answer's `hole` is null and its summary says the hole was cut but not read back.
