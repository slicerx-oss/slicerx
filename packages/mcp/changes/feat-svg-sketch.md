### Added

- `slicerx_design_sketch_svg` 1.0.0 turns SVG text into a finished sketch in the design timeline of the project open in the app, as Import SVG in the sketch tool does. Each closed path of a filled or stroked shape becomes a loop: straight runs are lines, circular arcs are three point arcs (`to`, `through`), a whole circle is a circle loop, and other curves are splines through points within `tolerance_mm` (0.01 when absent). Holes stay as loops inside and transforms apply. `scale` is mm per SVG unit (the file's own size when absent); without it `fit_width_mm` or `fit_height_mm` sizes the artwork, and `center` places its middle in sketch mm (the origin when absent). `frame` puts it on a plane or face as for `slicerx_design_sketch`. The reply has the sketch `id` and `name`, its `loops` as stored, `counts` of lines, arcs, splines and circles, `sizeMm`, `mmPerUnit`, the sketch `check` (each loop's role, the area and any issues) and `warnings` for what was left out (open strokes with no fill, text, images). A curve too dense to draw is refused before anything reaches the timeline. Pass the id as `sketch` to `slicerx_geom_extrude` or `slicerx_geom_revolve`.
