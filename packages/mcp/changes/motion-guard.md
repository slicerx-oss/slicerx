### Changed

- `slicerx_printer_gcode` and `slicerx_printer_filament`: the hub sends a raw line that can move the head under its motion guard. A move, home, probe or leveling command (`G29` and its decimals, `G30`, `G34`, `G80`, `G81`, `M48`, `BED_MESH_CALIBRATE`, `PROBE`, `QUAD_GANTRY_LEVEL`, `Z_TILT_ADJUST`, `SCREWS_TILT_CALCULATE` and the like, in any case, spacing or line number form), a start, filament or tool change macro (`PRINT_START`, `M600`, `M701`, `LOAD_FILAMENT`, `T1`), a position change other than `G92 E0`, and any code or macro the hub does not know as safe goes only while the printer is idle or finished, and only when the plate is known to be clear or the person says so on the card for that bed epoch; otherwise it fails with `bad_state` or `bed_check`. The card's work has `motion: true` and asks about the plate. Heater, fan, message, report and wait lines (`M104`, `M140`, `M106`, `M117`, `G4` and the like) go as before, also mid-print.
