### Added

- `slicerx_printer_filament_change` 1.0.0 asks a person to change the filament (M600) on its own card, action `printer.filament_change` with work kind `filament_change`. The printer parks the head and waits for the swap. The hub sends it while a print runs or is paused; on an idle or finished printer the person also says the plate is clear, and otherwise it fails with `bed_check`. It is refused before any card while the printer is preparing, in an error or offline. A raw plain `M600` line (at most a `B` word) from `slicerx_printer_gcode` takes the same path. A phone can only deny it.

### Changed

- `slicerx_printer_gcode` and `slicerx_printer_filament`: the hub sends a raw line by what it can do, and fails closed. Only `;` starts a comment, and parameters are read by letter, not by word. A line that can move the head goes only while the printer is idle or finished, and only when the plate is known to be clear or the person says so on the card for that bed epoch; otherwise it fails with `bad_state` or `bed_check`, and the card's work has `motion: true` and asks about the plate. That covers a move, home, probe or leveling command (`G29` and its decimals, `G30`, `G34`, `G80`, `G81`, `M48`, `BED_MESH_CALIBRATE`, `PROBE`, `QUAD_GANTRY_LEVEL`, `Z_TILT_ADJUST`, `SCREWS_TILT_CALCULATE` and the like, in any case, spacing or line number form), `G91`, a start, filament load or tool change macro (`PRINT_START`, `M701`, `LOAD_FILAMENT`, `T1`), `M109`, a `G92` with any letter but `E` (`G92 E0Z0` included), an `M600` with any parameter but `B`, a message carrying a host action (`M118 action:resume`, `RESPOND ... action:cancel`), any line with `(` or `)` or text after a checksum, and any code or macro the hub does not know as safe. Lines that cannot move the head but change a running print (`G21`, `G90`, `G92 E0`, `M73`, `M82`, `M83`, `M110`, `M190`, `M191`, `M400`, `TEMPERATURE_WAIT`, a `G4` of at most 60 s, and `M220` or `M221` with one `S` from 50 to 150) go only while the printer is idle or finished, with no plate question, and fail with `bad_state` mid-print. A longer `G4`, or any other `M220` or `M221`, fails with `bad_request`. Heater, fan, message and report lines (`M104`, `M140`, `M106`, `M117`, `M105` and the like) go as before, also mid-print. A plain `M600` is a filament change, which goes mid-print, see `slicerx_printer_filament_change`. A raw `RESUME` or `EXCLUDE_OBJECT` refused mid-print names Resume or Skip object.
