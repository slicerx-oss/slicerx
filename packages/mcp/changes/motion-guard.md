### Added

- `slicerx_printer_filament_change` 1.0.0 asks a person to change the filament (M600) on its own card, action `printer.filament_change` with work kind `filament_change`. The printer parks the head and waits for the swap. The hub sends it while a print runs or is paused; on an idle or finished printer the person also says the plate is clear, and otherwise it fails with `bed_check`. It is refused before any card while the printer is preparing, in an error or offline. A raw `M600` line from `slicerx_printer_gcode` takes the same path. A phone can only deny it.

### Changed

- `slicerx_printer_gcode` and `slicerx_printer_filament`: the hub sends a raw line that can move the head under its motion guard (a raw `M600` is a filament change, which goes mid-print, see `slicerx_printer_filament_change`; a raw `RESUME` or `EXCLUDE_OBJECT` refused mid-print names the resume or skip action). A move, home, probe or leveling command (`G29` and its decimals, `G30`, `G34`, `G80`, `G81`, `M48`, `BED_MESH_CALIBRATE`, `PROBE`, `QUAD_GANTRY_LEVEL`, `Z_TILT_ADJUST`, `SCREWS_TILT_CALCULATE` and the like, in any case, spacing or line number form), a start, filament load or tool change macro (`PRINT_START`, `M701`, `LOAD_FILAMENT`, `T1`), a position change other than `G92 E0`, and any code or macro the hub does not know as safe goes only while the printer is idle or finished, and only when the plate is known to be clear or the person says so on the card for that bed epoch; otherwise it fails with `bad_state` or `bed_check`. The card's work has `motion: true` and asks about the plate. Heater, fan, message, report and wait lines (`M104`, `M140`, `M106`, `M117`, `G4` and the like) go as before, also mid-print.
