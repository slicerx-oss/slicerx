### Changed

- `slicerx_design_new` 1.5.0 (from 1.4.0): an exact design on an sx-geom that runs once per call, where each body goes back as text between calls, has `solidLimits` in its reply: `textChars`, the longest solid text a part keeps (8 MiB of text, as before), and the counts sx-geom refuses to save a body as text past, `edges` (3,300) and `controlPoints` (178,000, the control points of its spline curves and surfaces). An exact design on a served sx-geom has no `solidLimits`: it holds its bodies by number, never loads them from text, and has no such limit. On the text path, a step that makes a part past either count says so with the count and the limit, for example "This part is now too detailed to stay an exact solid, so from this step on it is built as a mesh. (3,412 edges, limit 3,300.)", and a design that must stay exact stops at that step with "this step would make the part too detailed to stay an exact solid (3,412 edges, limit 3,300)." The text limit itself stays at 8 MiB.
