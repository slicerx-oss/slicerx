### Fixed

- `slicerx_geom_fit_check` no longer reports a touch between facets that lie in one plane but stay apart, such as the facets of a hinge pin's knuckles on one axis and radius with a 0.6 mm gap between them. Whether a facet edge meets a facet is now decided with exact orientation tests. Before, rounding could put the edge on the facet, and a hinge box drawn with 0.6 mm gaps read "1 piece; Base and Lid touch at (16.6, 40.3, 29.8), 0.0 mm" instead of 2 pieces, 0.58 mm apart. The same applies to the `pieces` and `gaps` items of `slicerx_design_check` and the clearance from `slicerx_geom_interference`. Faces that rest flat on each other are now found touching by the same exact test, so the point named for such a touch can be another place where they meet; the piece count and the gaps stay the same.
