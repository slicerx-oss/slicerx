### Fixed

- `slicerx_design_edit_step` 1.6.2 (from 1.6.1): suppressing a boolean keeps every part. The parts the boolean took in stay in the design, moved to the end of the parts list, and every later step stays on the part it was made on. Build A and B, union them, build C, then suppress the union: the parts are A, C and B. Before, B was gone with no message, because replay made C again in B's place. Turning the boolean back on gives the parts as they were.
- `slicerx_design_steps` 1.6.1 (from 1.6.0): the form lines follow the parts as replay numbers them. After a suppressed boolean every line read "made outside the history"; now each says how its part was made. A design whose history starts from parts it already had (its base) also gets a line per part made by a step, where before every line read "made outside the history".
- `slicerx_design_open` 1.3.1 (from 1.3.0): a saved design with a suppressed boolean reopens with the parts the boolean took in, at the end of the parts list. A file saved while those parts were missing gets them back.
- `slicerx_geom_extrude` 1.12.1 (from 1.12.0): a join that raises a body lifts the parts that stood on it at the steps that made them, read as replay numbers the parts. Before, after a boolean, a later part could be lifted at the step of a part the boolean took in. `slicerx_design_edit_step` re-seats parts on a reshaped loft the same way.
- Every tool that replays a design numbers its parts the same way. Parts added to a design (a clone, a merged object) stay at the end of the parts list when it replays or opens again.
- Not covered: deleting a boolean. A deleted step leaves nothing to number the parts from, so a new body made after it can still take the place of a part the boolean took in. Suppress the boolean instead to keep every part.
