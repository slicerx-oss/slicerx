### Changed

- `slicerx_geom_loft` 1.9.0 (from 1.8.0) takes `exact`: with `exact: true` and no design, the loft is also made as an exact solid, and the answer gains `bodySmb`, the solid as the text the engine's `brep.load` reads. The STL and every other field of the answer are the ones the loft gives without it. When the solid is not made the answer has `exact` with `made: false` and `why`, one sentence saying what stood in the way; the loft itself is never refused because of the ask. For now the solid is made for a new body through profiles of straight lines, in a build with the exact kernel. With `design` the tool does not read `exact`: the step is added as before, the answer has no `exact`, `bodySmb` or other word on it, and the design's own replay decides whether its body is exact. Without a design the summary ends ", with its exact solid" or ", without an exact solid".
