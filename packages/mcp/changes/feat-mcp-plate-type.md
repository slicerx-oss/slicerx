### Changed

- `slicerx_slice_file` 1.14.0 and `slicerx_estimate_file` 1.11.0 take `plate_type`, the build plate the print goes on, by the names the app's plate pickers show: Textured PEI, Smooth PEI, Cool plate, Engineering plate, High temp plate, Textured cool plate or Supertack plate, in any letter case. The slice heats the bed to that plate's first layer and other layer temperatures; a filament that sets none for the plate gets the settings defaults the app shows for it. `plate_type` goes over the plate type a project, a project's plate or a profile sets. A name no plate has is refused with `invalid_input`, and the message lists the names (`plate_type: no plate is called "Glass". Use one of: Textured PEI, ...`). The answer gives the plate back in `plate_type` and a `Plate type:` line. A slice without `plate_type` is unchanged. `slicerx_arrange_file` does not take it, since the layout is the same on any plate, and names it in an `ignored_arguments` warning.
- A `curr_bed_type` override is still refused with `invalid_settings`; the message now says to pass the plate with `plate_type` and lists the names.
