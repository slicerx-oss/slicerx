### Changed

- `slicerx_geom_build` 1.3.0 (from 1.2.0), `slicerx_geom_extrude` 1.8.0 (from 1.7.0), `slicerx_geom_revolve` 1.7.0 (from 1.6.0), `slicerx_geom_loft` 1.5.0 (from 1.4.0) and `slicerx_geom_sweep` 1.8.0 (from 1.7.0) on a design: a new part (from `slicerx_geom_build`, or `operation` new) whose `feature` is a piece that a print-in-place plan tied to the design names in `printInPlace` takes that piece's name, spelled as the plan spells it, so `slicerx_design_check` finds the piece. Before, a plan that named its pieces after the features building them, such as Base shell and Lid shell, with the parts built as Base and Lid, failed its check on the names alone. The name given stays when it is already one of that plan's pieces, when another part of the design has the piece's name, when no tied plan names the feature, or when the plan's sizes changed after it was approved. The summary says so, such as "The new part is named Base shell, as the plan names that print-in-place piece, in place of Base", and the saved history keeps the new name (a `slicerx_geom_build` step is named after it, such as Add Base shell). A plan is tied to a design by `slicerx_design_check` with `design`, and in the mimir tab by the design its part is built in.
