### Changed

- `slicerx_geom_array` 1.7.0 (from 1.6.0) takes `onSurface: true` on a row (`kind` linear) that repeats `steps`: each copy slides onto the part's surface where it lands and turns to it, so a row of slits, windows or dents along a curved side keeps the first one's depth. A copy whose place meets no surface, or a surface turned past the side, refuses the call with `Copy N:` and why, and the design is unchanged. Without `steps` it is refused.
- `slicerx_geom_subtract` 1.3.0 (from 1.2.1) refuses a cut on a design that would take away what another part stands on (its bottom at the cut's top, the cut reaching deeper, and the cut taking more than a quarter of the footprint it stands on, read from its triangles, so a notch at the edge of a lid's seat or a hole inside a ring's opening goes ahead), naming that part and its box. `slicerx_geom_build` 1.5.0 (from 1.4.0) refuses a new part off the bed that stands where another part already stands on the same face and rises above it, naming that part and its box. A part joined at another height, or parts laid out together on the bed, are not held.
- `slicerx_geom_build` 1.5.0 with a design also holds each solid of a new part on its own against the parts standing on the same face, and reads a standing part's footprint from its triangles rather than its box. Pieces that each stand clear are no longer refused for the box around them all (two end posts either side of a lamp, a part made of strips around an open area). A solid that does stand in another part is refused by its index, with what clears it: the z to start it at on that part's top, or the least move in x or y beside it. A part with a solid other than a box or cylinder is read whole, as before.
