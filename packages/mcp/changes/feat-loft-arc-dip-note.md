### Changed

- `slicerx_geom_loft` 1.13.0 (from 1.12.3) names a section whose arc dips under the section's lowest drawn point, for a new body or a join on a design: the summary ends with a note for the deepest such section, such as "Section 4 of 9 dips 0.12 mm under its lowest drawn point: the arc ending at (6.72, 0) bottoms out before it gets there, and the body follows it. Move that arc's through point so it runs level into (6.72, 0), tangent to the line it meets, or draw that stretch as a line.", and the answer gains `arcDips`, each section's place, the section count, the dip in mm and the arc's end nearest the dip, deepest first. A dip of 0.01 mm or less is not named. The loft is never refused or changed for it. Arcs drawn through a point or by a center and a sweep are read; a section with an arc drawn by its radius alone, a cut, a thickened loft and an open chain get no note. Replaying a design gives no note.
