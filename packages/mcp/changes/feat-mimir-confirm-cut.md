### Changed

- `slicerx_design_plan` 1.6.0, in the mimir tab, waits for the person's Go only when its sizes must fit something (a sheet with `fits` in the run) or its pieces print assembled and move. Any other plan, at its default size, shows on its card and is approved as shown at once, with `auto: true` in the answer. Amends follow the same rule.
