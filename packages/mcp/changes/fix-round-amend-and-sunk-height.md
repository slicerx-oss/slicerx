### Fixed

- `slicerx_design_plan` 1.23.0 (from 1.22.0), in the mimir tab, refuses a build's amend that adds a feature while the plan's fix round is open (a check of it failed and the check that ends the round has not come): "add: the fix round is open, so this amend can't add a feature; Mainmast base would need its own build step. Fix the failed items on the part as planned. To move a planned feature onto another one, send that feature with its on changed and no add.". The plan stays as it was and no card shows, so the round's one amend is still there to use. Before, the amend was taken, and the next check failed its feature count, since nothing in the round built the added feature: a ship plan amended with "Mainmast base" failed `feature count (want 11, got 10 steps for 11 planned features)`. An amend before the first failed check, and a change the person asked for (`asked`), still add features. The description says an amend adds no feature while the fix round is open.
- `slicerx_design_put_plan` 1.12.0 (from 1.11.0) refuses the same way, with the same words, an amend of your own plan that adds a feature while its fix round is open. Before, such an amend was taken, so the fix round could still add a feature nothing in it builds. A size change in the round, and an amend before the first failed check, are taken as before.
- `slicerx_design_check` 1.29.0 (from 1.28.0) gives the exact fix for a stacked part sunk into what it stands on when its height is planned. Inside the sides of what it stands on: "Forecastle deck stands on Hull, but its base sits 0.61 mm below Hull's top, so 2.39 mm of it shows where the plan has 2 mm. Hull's lowest point under it is at z 12.61 mm. With design.edit_step, set the size of the step that makes Forecastle deck to 2.61 mm along z (it grows up from its base at z 12), or move that step by [0, 0, 0.61] and set its size to 2 mm along z." Past those sides, where the sunk part shows, it says so and gives the move alone: "Mainmast stands on Promenade deck, but its base sits 1.1 mm below Promenade deck's top, so 12.9 mm of it shows where the plan has 14 mm, and its sunk part shows past Promenade deck's sides. Build it up from Promenade deck's top, whose highest point is at z 15 mm: with design.edit_step, move the step that makes Mainmast by [0, 0, 1.1] and set its size to 14 mm along z." Before, it said only to build the part from the top, so a fix round had to guess the height.
- `slicerx_design_check` 1.29.0 holds a part sunk into what it stands on, inside its sides, to the same height check a part seated on the top gets: its planned height showing above the top within 1.35 times. Before, it had to show within a layer (0.2 mm) of the plan, so a ship's forecastle deck sunk 0.61 mm into the hull with 2.39 mm of a planned 2 mm showing failed, where the same deck seated on the hull with 2.39 mm showing passed. A part sunk past its support's sides still fails, since its sunk part shows there.
