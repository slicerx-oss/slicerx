### Changed

- `slicerx_slice_file` 1.16.0 and `slicerx_estimate_file` 1.13.0 check a Bambu Lab H3C plate printed by object with the H3C's own toolhead instead of the P1 family's, and check every tool change: the trip to the PrintTool rack across the front, the docks of the two hotends the change swaps (as the slice's filament map puts the filaments on hotends), and the trip to the purge chute at the back right. Such a plate can now be refused with `collision`, or answered with `collisions`, for a `tool_change` or `dock` collision it did not have before, and the depth of a `hotend` collision can differ. A change G-code that lifts over `max_layer_z`, as the maker's does, still clears everything printed before.
- `slicerx_arrange_file` 1.2.0 (in `check`) and `slicerx_project_show` 1.3.0 (in `byObject.engine`, with `check: true`) answer heimdall's plate check of an H3C plate printed by object with the H3C's own toolhead, so its `hotend` and `nozzle` collisions and their depths can differ. Tool change trips stay in `unchecked` there, as before: only the slice checks them.
- Checked and left alone: the other file and project tools answer no heimdall collisions, and `slicerx_app_ui` and `slicerx_app_collision_show` read back what the app itself shows, so they move with the app, not with this server. Plates printed by layer and other printers are unchanged.
