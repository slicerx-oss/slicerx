### Changed

- `slicerx_design_put_plan` 1.3.0 returns `underFloors`: each planned size under the smallest the printer makes, with `feature`, `size`, `planned`, `floor` and `why`, plus `pin` and `bore` for a gap around a pin. A gap is a size on each side. It is held to the gap `slicerx_design_check` holds a print-in-place part to, for the nozzle, layer height and material: above or below only when the pieces print in place (named in `printInPlace`, counted by a size beside a gap size, or stated as print in place, printed assembled, captive, articulated or flexi), side by side around a hinge or pin printed apart, and not at all otherwise. A wall or pin is held to two nozzle lines and a hole to the nozzle. The summary names each one. The plan is kept as given; put it again with the floor to build to it. A plan with a hinge or a pin piece that does not say whether it prints assembled on its hinge (`printInPlace`) or laid apart (an assumption, or `printInPlace` false) is kept too, and the result gives `unstated` with what to say. A plan that says both, assembled and apart, gets `unstated` telling it to say one way.
