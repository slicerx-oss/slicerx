### Changed

- `slicerx_multicolor_assign` 0.2.0 (from 0.1.0) no longer says there is no paint API. Its answer has `howToApply` in place of `blocked`: paint a design's bands and regions with `slicerx_paint_preview` and `slicerx_paint_apply` (a height band, or faces facing a direction, in a filament) after setting its filaments, or paint any other object with the paint brush. It is still a plan and changes nothing.
- `slicerx_paint_preview` 1.2.0 (from 1.1.0): a height band given no `max_depth` splits triangles deep enough that its edges land within 0.04 mm of the heights asked (depth 6 to 10, by the tallest triangle), and the step keeps that depth. Before, depth 6 put the edge of a band on a tall face up to a fifth of a millimeter off, so a 1 mm band printed one layer more of its color. A `max_depth` given is used as before.
