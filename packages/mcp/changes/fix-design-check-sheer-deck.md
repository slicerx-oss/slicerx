### Fixed

- `slicerx_design_check` 1.24.0 (from 1.23.0): the "stands on" item reads the deck under a stacked part when its support is a loft whose top rises or falls along its length, such as a ship's hull with a sheer. The part is held to the lowest point of that deck under its footprint, which is where a flat base meets it with no gap, read straight between the loft's sections. Before, the part was held to the support's highest point anywhere, so a seated part read as sunk by the height of the rise at the far end. A deckhouse seated at 15.9 mm on the 16 mm stretch of a deck that rises to 19 mm at the stern read "its base is 3.1 mm below its top, so 1 mm of it shows of a planned 4.1 mm"; it now passes. Decks seated at 15.95 mm on a 16 mm midship line, with the bow rising to 17.8 mm, read 1.85 mm below; they now pass. A part sunk 3 mm into the deck under it still fails. A support with one top height (an extruded deck, or a loft whose sections are level on top) reads as before, and so does a part whose footprint the steps do not give (a merged array, a revolve, text). For a design with such a loft, the result's `featureHeights` also gives the support's top at each section (`deck`) and each part's footprint (`foot`).
