### Fixed

- `slicerx_geom_info` 1.2.0 and `slicerx_design_check` 1.1.0 read whether a design's part is closed the way the slicer welds it: corners at the same single-precision point count as one vertex, whatever their triangle indices. Before, both read closure by index, so two surfaces that only touched (a swept bar's end on a post, say) read as closed while the slicer found edges with four faces there, and a part whose triangles shared no corners read as open. A part that is not closed now says why: `slicerx_geom_info` per part `edges` (`boundaryEdges`, `nonManifoldEdges`, `flippedEdges`) come from the welded reading and its summary reads `not closed (1 edge where more than two faces meet)`, and the `watertight` item of `slicerx_design_check` reads `open: ... (surfaces that touch without joining, which repair leaves as they are); rebuild that join so the shapes overlap a little`, with `body.openWhy` beside it. A model given as an STL reads as before.
