### Changed

- `slicerx_slice_file` 1.17.0 and `slicerx_estimate_file` 1.14.0 check each tool change of a Bambu Lab H2C plate printed by object at the hotends it swaps, as the slice's filament map puts the filaments on the left nozzle and the rack's hotends, instead of by filament number. A change between two filaments on two rack hotends is checked at the rack, and a change between two filaments on one hotend, or to the hotend already in the head, is checked at the chute only. Such a plate can now be refused with `collision`, or answered with `collisions`, for a `tool_change` or `dock` collision at the rack it did not have before, or lose one at the rack the printer never visits.
- Checked and left alone: `slicerx_arrange_file` and `slicerx_project_show` answer heimdall's plate check, which leaves tool change trips to the slice (`unchecked`), and the H2C's heads do not change, so their answers stay the same. No other tool answers with heimdall's collisions of a file.
