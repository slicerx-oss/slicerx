## [0.2.8] - 2026-11-08

### Added

- Every modeling tool that makes or changes a body takes `feature`, the plan feature the step builds, by its name in the plan: `slicerx_geom_extrude` and `slicerx_geom_revolve` 1.4.0, `slicerx_geom_push_pull` 1.3.0, `slicerx_geom_loft`, `slicerx_geom_sweep`, `slicerx_geom_boolean`, `slicerx_geom_fillet`, `slicerx_geom_chamfer`, `slicerx_geom_shell`, `slicerx_geom_cut` and `slicerx_geom_split` 1.2.0, `slicerx_geom_hole` 1.2.1 (past 1.1.1, which says it never makes a new hole, since both changed it), `slicerx_geom_array` 1.2.1, and `slicerx_geom_repair`, `slicerx_geom_hollow`, `slicerx_geom_emboss`, `slicerx_geom_build`, `slicerx_geom_subtract` and `slicerx_geom_mirror` 1.1.0. With `design` it is recorded on the step and kept when the design is saved and opened again. `slicerx_design_steps` 1.1.0 reads it back on each step that has one, and `slicerx_design_edit_step` 1.1.0 sets it (empty clears it).
- `slicerx_design_trace_image` 1.0.0 traces a PNG or JPEG into a finished sketch in the design timeline of the project open in the app, as Trace image in the sketch tool does. `image` is `{path}` (a .png, .jpg or .jpeg file inside the directories the server may read; its type, after links are followed, and its size are checked before it is read) or `{base64}`, at most 30 MB and not empty. The image is traced on the server, so its size is not limited by the app link; the loops then go to the timeline as `slicerx_design_sketch` sends them. `mode` picks the inside: `auto` (opaque areas when the image has transparency, else the dark side of an automatic split), `dark` or `light` against `threshold` (0 to 255, automatic when absent), `color` near any of `colors` (up to 8, `#rrggbb`) within `color_tolerance` (0 to 100, 12 when absent), or `alpha`. Specks and pinholes under `speck_mm2` (0.5 when absent) are cleaned away. Edges become lines, arcs, circles and splines within `smoothing_mm` (0.01 to 10, half a working pixel when absent); `fit` is `lines`, `arcs` or `curves` (the default), and a turn sharper than `corner_angle_deg` (1 to 179, 50 when absent) is a corner. One of `width_mm`, `height_mm` or `mm_per_px` sizes the trace (50 mm wide when none is given), `center` places its middle in sketch mm (when absent, the middle of the plane: on the bed, the middle of the app's bed while the app is linked, else this server's bed, else the origin; on a plane or face, the frame's origin), `frame` puts it on a plane or face as for `slicerx_design_sketch`, and `max_side_px` (16 to 4096, 2048 when absent) is the size the image is shrunk to before tracing. `dry_run` traces and reports without adding anything, so a client can tune the settings first; `preview_image` also returns a PNG of the image with the loops drawn over it. The reply has the sketch `id` and `name` (none on a dry run), the `loops`, `counts` of lines, arcs, splines and circles, `sizeMm`, `mmPerPx`, `imagePx`, `workingPx`, `dropped` (specks and pinholes cleaned away), the `mode` used and its `threshold`, the sketch `check` and `warnings`. The loops never cross and none is left out: where a fit would cross, it is fitted tighter or kept as traced, and a trace that cannot be kept apart within the segments a sketch takes, or with an outline too small to draw, is refused. A trace of more than 256 loops, a loop longer than a sketch loop takes (512 pieces, 4096 points, a spline through 512), loops over the 63.5 KB the app link carries, or a spline that cannot be drawn, is refused with the setting to raise, and a dry run reports it in `refused`; every refusal names the option to change (`speck_mm2`, `smoothing_mm`, `color_tolerance`, `max_side_px`). Pass the id as `sketch` to `slicerx_geom_extrude` or `slicerx_geom_revolve`.
- `slicerx_app_layer_profile` 1.0.0 reads a plate's own layer heights in the running app (the shown plate, or `plate` by number or id): `mode`, what the plate slices with (`calibration`, `resume`, `own`, `sleipnir` or `fixed`; a calibration plate and a resume plan keep their own layers over a profile; a resume plan holds only on the plate it was planned for, until that plate or its settings change; after that the mode is `resume_void` and the plate does not slice until the plan is made again or the app's Print everything again clears it), `resume` (the kept plan: `kept`, its `plate` number and whether it `holds`), `profile` as [height above the bed mm, layer height mm] points or null, the printer's `limits` (`min_mm`, `max_mm`, `first_layer_mm`), the plate's `top_mm`, the `layers` the profile gives, `layer_height_mm` and a `revision`. `slicerx_project_layer_profile` 1.0.0 sets one (`action: set` with `points`) or clears it (`action: reset`), as one toolbar Undo step, and returns it read back. Heights outside the printer's limits are refused with `invalid_input`; an optional `revision` from the read refuses an edit after another change with `busy`, as does an edit while the app loads, slices or edits. Both need the app link; the app link gains `layers.profile.get` (read) and `layers.profile.edit` (edit).
- `slicerx_design_sketch_project` 1.0.0 projects model geometry onto a finished sketch's plane as sketch curves: picked edges (`kind` `edges`, `edges` from `slicerx_geom_edge_pick`, chained where they meet), a face's outline with its holes (`kind` `face`, `face` as for `slicerx_geom_push_pull`) or a body's whole outline seen along the sketch normal (`kind` `silhouette`), from the body `design`. `mode` `real` (the default) makes them profile curves for `slicerx_geom_extrude` and `slicerx_geom_revolve`; `reference` keeps them as construction curves that make no profile. An extrude or revolve made from the sketch before a projection never takes it, so projecting never changes an existing solid. With `projection` it switches one's `mode` or, with `remove`, takes it out with its curves; a removed projection's id is never given to a later one, and a sketch that has given out ids up to `p999999` refuses a new projection with too many projections. A source is read as the body stands at the sketch's place in the timeline. The curves follow their source when the design changes earlier in the timeline, and the bodies drawn on the sketch rebuild; a source that is gone, or a follow the bodies drawn on the sketch cannot take, keeps its curves and is reported as `lost`. Returns every projection of the sketch with its `kind`, `body`, `mode`, `loops` and `lost`, and how many bodies rebuilt. Runs on the project open in the app; app link method `design.sketch_project` (edit class).
- `slicerx_app_presets`, `slicerx_app_preset_compare`, `slicerx_app_preset_copy` and `slicerx_app_preset_undo` 1.0.0: the presets the person saved in the open app, through the app link. `slicerx_app_presets` lists them (`id`, `kind`, `name`, `parent`, `settings`, `inUse`; optional `kind`). `slicerx_app_preset_compare` compares one (`preset`, by id or name) with another saved preset (`with`) or `against` `"parent"` or `"stock"`, with `all`, and returns the rows Compare presets lists, each side read over the stock settings of the printer on the plate. `slicerx_app_preset_copy` copies one setting (`key`) from the second side into the preset, as the panel's copy button does, and answers with `value` (what the preset gives now), `kept` and an `undoId`; `slicerx_app_preset_undo` puts only that setting back, refused with `not_supported` (`details.reason` `changed`) once the setting changed again, and `not_found` for an undo id used or unknown or a deleted preset. The person approves each copy and undo in the app (a decline is `not_supported` with `details.reason` `declined`), and the call waits up to 2 minutes for them. Copy and undo are `slice` class, so they ask nothing on the client: the app's dialog, which names the setting and the value, is the person's approval of each write. They need the app link's edit grant, and a client allowed to read only is refused with `not_supported` (`details.appLink` `read_only`) before the person is asked (link methods `presets.copy` and `presets.undo`, edit; `presets.list` and `presets.compare`, read).
- `slicerx_preset_compare` 1.0.0: compares two presets of the same kind (process, filament or printer) side by side, or one with the maker preset it inherits from (`against: "parent"`) or with the stock settings (`against: "stock"`), as Compare presets in the app does. Each side is a profile id or name (`a`, `b`) or a preset file (`a_file`, `b_file`). Returns the settings that differ, or every setting of the kind with `all: true` (each marked `differs`), in `groups` by settings group in the app's order, each row with its `label`, `key`, both values and both as the app writes them (`aText`, `bText`); G-code settings also carry `lines` (lines added and removed, and a unified diff, from the line diff the project G-code review uses). Printer connection details and hidden bookkeeping keys are never listed. A setting a side leaves out reads as its default for the kind, and so does the stock side; `slicerx_app_preset_compare` reads over the stock settings of the printer on the plate instead. Every refusal has a code and `details.reason`: `no_first`, `two_firsts`, `no_second`, `two_seconds`, `kinds_differ`, `several_presets` and `no_parent` (`invalid_input`), `unknown_profile` and `parent_not_shipped` (`unknown_profile`).
- `slicerx_open_in_app` 1.3.0 opens a textured OBJ with its texture: the desktop app reads the MTL files the OBJ names and the PNG or JPEG images they name from the OBJ's own folder (plain relative names, subfolders allowed, never a parent folder, another drive, or a file reached through a symbolic link or junction; at most 64 files and 256 MB in all) and paints the model from its texture as it opens. For an OBJ that names an MTL the answer has `texture`, with `found` and `missing` files by the names the OBJ and MTL give them, and the summary names what is missing. Then `slicerx_app_paint_from_texture` paints it again, for example after the filaments change.
- `slicerx_app_paint_from_texture` 1.0.0: previews color paint from an object's texture in the open app, without changing it: the texture the object was opened with (an OBJ opened together with its MTL and images, or a 3MF with `texture2d` groups). `mode` `nearest` (the default) gives each bit of surface the filament nearest the texture's color there; `palette` picks as many colors as there are filaments from the texture and sets them as the filament colors, and is refused while a filament color is set by hand or by the printer. Answers with a preview id, `changed` (true when applying it changes the paint, a part's own filament or the filament colors), `filaments` (slot, area in mm2, color), `partSlots` (`part` index, `name` and `slot`: the filament with the most area becomes that part's own; parts that share a name keep every filament as paint instead and are not listed), the paint regions and `notes`. `slicerx_app_paint_apply` applies it as one Undo step, with the same `changed`, and `slicerx_app_paint` reads it back. Refused with `not_found` for an object opened without a texture, or whose texture paints nothing. App link method `paint.from_texture` (read class).
- `slicerx_design_open` 1.2.0 takes `texture` (`filaments` as `#rrggbb`, slot 1 first, up to 32, and `mode` `nearest` or `palette`): a 3MF's `texture2d` groups paint each part's color layer, the filament with the most area becomes the part's slot, and each design opened that way returns `texture` (`colors`, `slots` with their areas in mm2, `palette`, `notes`). Without it a file opens as before.
- `slicerx_design_sketch_svg` 1.0.0 turns SVG text (the call's arguments may come to 63.5 KB, 65024 bytes of UTF-8 JSON with quotes escaped and every field counted, the most the app link carries less a 512 byte margin; text in other scripts counts 2 to 4 bytes a character; a larger call is refused with its size and a hint to simplify the SVG or import it in parts) into a finished sketch in the design timeline of the project open in the app, as Import SVG in the sketch tool does. Each closed path of a filled or stroked shape becomes a loop: straight runs are lines, circular arcs are three point arcs (`to`, `through`), a whole circle is a circle loop, and other curves are splines through points within `tolerance_mm` (0.001 to 1, 0.01 when absent). Holes stay as loops inside and transforms apply. `scale` is mm per SVG unit (the file's own size when absent); without it `fit_width_mm` or `fit_height_mm` sizes the artwork, and `center` places its middle in sketch mm. Without `center` the middle lands on the middle of the plane, as the sketch tool places it: the bed's middle on the bed, the frame's origin on a plane or face. `frame` puts it on a plane or face as for `slicerx_design_sketch`. The reply has the sketch `id` and `name`, its `loops` as stored, `counts` of lines, arcs, splines and circles, `sizeMm`, `mmPerUnit`, the sketch `check` (each loop's role, the area and any issues) and `warnings` for what was left out (open strokes with no fill, text, images). A spline that cannot be drawn (too dense, or repeated points) is refused before anything reaches the timeline, as for `slicerx_design_sketch`. Pass the id as `sketch` to `slicerx_geom_extrude` or `slicerx_geom_revolve`.

A phone can only deny it.

- `slicerx_printer_set_slot` 1.0.0 asks a person to set one filament slot, such as an AMS slot: `setting` `{slot, material, color, filamentId, nozzleTempMin, nozzleTempMax}` (`material` one of the listed filament types, any case; `color` `#rrggbb`; `filamentId` the slot's name, which is the printer's filament preset id, 1 to 16 characters; nozzle range 150 to 350 °C, low first), with an optional `reason` shown on the card. - `slicerx_geom_sweep` 1.0.0: a solid swept from one closed sketch profile (`profile`, `frame` and `loops` as for `slicerx_geom_revolve`) along an open path (`path`: one open chain of lines and arcs on a plane as `frame` and `loops`, or an open polyline in space as `points`), as a new body (`operation` `new`), joined to a target or cut from it. The profile keeps its place and turn relative to the path and does not twist about it, with straight sides between path points and flat ends square to the path. A straight path sweeps exactly the extrusion. Refused with a plain sentence: a profile that is open, has holes or is more than one loop; a path that is closed, crosses itself or doubles back; a profile whose plane is within 1 degree of parallel to the path's start; a bend tighter than the profile can follow; and a body that runs into itself where the path comes back near itself. With `design` it adds a `sketch.sweep` step (named "Sweep", or "Sweep cut") that replays and saves in the 3MF like every other step, and takes finished sketch ids as `profile_sketch` and `path_sketch`; without one it writes an STL with volume, bounds and whether it is watertight. App link method `geom.sweep` (edit class).
- `slicerx_arrange_file` 1.0.0: packs the distinct parts of one plate of a 3MF or .sx3mf project (or of a model file) on the printer's bed with the engine's arrange (`sx arrange`), without slicing. The layout keeps off the printer's keep-out zones (`bed_exclude_area` and the A1 family's nozzle wrap check corner), keeps the extruder clearance radius plus each part's brim, skirt and support reach between parts when the plate prints by object (parts taller than the gantry rod in lanes of their own when the plate check asks for it), keeps `spacing_mm` (6 by default) by layer, and leaves room for the prime tower in two filaments or more. Parts keep their rotation and scale and may turn about Z (`rotate`). It returns each part's `center_mm`, `turn_deg` and `transform`, the parts with no room in `not_fit` (`no_room` or `too_large`, never overlapped, `fits` false), and printing by object the plate check of the layout. The same plate always gives the same layout. sx engine only.
- `slicerx_slice_file` 1.6.0 and `slicerx_estimate_file` 1.4.0 take `placement` `arrange`: the plate's parts are packed on the bed first, as `slicerx_arrange_file` lays them out, and the result's `arranged` gives the layout. "Enabled" skips those retractions, "Disabled" keeps them, and "Auto" skips them when every filament's `filament_metal_stickiness` is low or untested (PLA) and keeps them otherwise (PETG). A file with only the old `reduce_infill_retraction` key reads as before. The app opens projects the same way.
- A travel through a printer's exclusion area or the A1's nozzle wrap check corner, or within the nozzle's width of one, is now refused with `collision` like a print path there, as a `keep_out` item in `details.collisions`. The server reads sx's sentence for it ("A print path or travel enters ...") as well as the older one.
- A Bambu Studio or OrcaSlicer project sliced with `project_settings` no longer fails on its stock start G-code (the Bambu Lab A1's `M211`, `M500` and `M18`). The project's printer G-code is compared with the printer's stock text, the template SlicerX ships or any version Bambu Studio or OrcaSlicer shipped for the model, and stock text is used as it is; the result's `project_gcode` says which. Other G-code stops the call with the new `project_gcode_review` code, with the diff against the printer profile's G-code and each flagged line in `structuredContent.error.details`. The new `project_gcode: "profile"` argument slices with the printer profile's G-code instead. No tool call can choose the project's own G-code.
- `slicerx_slice_file` and `slicerx_estimate_file` slice a 3MF plate as its objects, so `print_sequence` "by object" prints each one before the next, with the nozzle lifted over the finished ones. Before, the plate became one mesh and printed layer by layer. A file is placed on the printer's own bed (`printable_area`), `project_settings` takes a plate's own print sequence, and `slicerx_inspect_project` lists it per plate. A by-object plate whose objects sit too close for the toolhead, or with an object the gantry or lid would hit, fails with the new `sequence_clearance` code.
- A user's preset that inherits from a maker profile SlicerX ships (`profile_files`, or a slot in `filaments`) no longer makes the whole slice use the strict G-code checks. G-code it inherits is the shipped text; only G-code the file sets itself is checked strictly. Before, a user's PLA preset on a Bambu Lab A1 stopped the slice with `preflight_blocked`.
- `slicerx_slice_file` and `slicerx_estimate_file` no longer refuse the start G-code of shipped printer profiles (the Bambu Lab A1's, for one). Like the app, they mark the custom G-code as trusted only when no G-code setting came from a profile SlicerX does not ship, a project file, a preset file or an override; anything else still gets the strict checks. Projects sliced through mimir (`slicerx_slice`) follow the same rule.
